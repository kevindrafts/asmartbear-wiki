# How to hire people who are better than you

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/hire-better-than-you/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Hiring beyond the founder's expertise is necessary to improve the company, yet makes direct technical evaluation difficult. Cohen looks for candidates who teach the interviewer, connect ideas to real constraints, ask revealing questions, and improve thinking beyond their own department. References become more useful when asked where a person thrives or struggles than when asked for generic endorsement.

**Apply it:** Discuss a real company problem and examine how the candidate reasons, identifies unknowns, and draws on direct experience. Test environmental fit through concrete reference examples.

**Boundary:** These signals do not prove expertise or eliminate hiring mistakes. Cohen's urgency about correcting poor hires assumes an actual mismatch, not merely a leader with an unfamiliar style.
