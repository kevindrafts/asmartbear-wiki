# How annual pre-pay creates an infinite marketing budget

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/annual-prepay/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Annual collection can change the timing of growth funding even when recurring revenue is unchanged. Cohen’s diagrams first lengthen payback by accounting for service costs, then show upfront receipts arriving before acquisition bills fall due. He stresses setting aside the money required to deliver the service.

**Apply it:** Compare collection timing, gross margin, acquisition cost, retention, and refund obligations under monthly and annual options.

**Boundary:** The headline’s unlimited budget is explicitly rhetorical: other expenses and cancellations remain. Annual buyers may already intend to stay, so their retention does not prove the plan caused loyalty. His cancellation adjustment is approximate, and his refund options are business-policy illustrations.
