# Marketing Platform Independence

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/marketing-platform-independence/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A platform can change the rules on which a dependent business relies. Cohen distinguishes a tool that is painful but possible to replace from a dependency whose removal destroys the business model. He recommends considering governance, incentives, portability, and diversification, using historical advertising and hosting examples.

**Apply it:** Describe the consequence if each critical provider or distribution channel changes terms or access, then identify a practical fallback.

**Boundary:** The favorable historical assessment of the WordPress ecosystem is the author's view at publication, not an assurance about present governance or relationships. Open source can aid portability without eliminating every ecosystem dependency.
