# Making plans when I can't predict anything

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/making-plans-when-i-can-t-predict-anything/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer combines a direction with adaptable execution. Cohen recommends testing assumptions with customers, using distinctive strengths, retaining alternative routes, and changing plans when evidence warrants it. Once a repeatable approach emerges, the organization should become better at executing it.

**Apply it:** Write the current customer and product hypothesis, the next test, and the evidence that would trigger a revision. Preserve options where the future remains uncertain.
