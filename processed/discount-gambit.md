# Discount gambit

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/discount-gambit/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen contrasts an immediate concession with protecting an early customer against a future price increase. Arbitrary deep discounts can teach buyers that the initial price was unreliable, invite further bargaining, and damage relationships when customers compare terms.

**Apply it:** Establish understandable pricing rules and consider how an incentive affects trust beyond the initial close.

**Boundary:** This is an argument from Cohen’s experience, not universal evidence against every discount. Historical claims about particular companies’ pricing policies should not be treated as current facts or a reason to invent future increases.
