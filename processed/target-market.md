# Your target market isn’t demographic

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/target-market/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that buying circumstances often describe a market better than company size or demographic labels. A team inside a large enterprise may buy the same simple tool as a small business without representing an enterprise-wide sales opportunity. Shared workflows, constraints, desired outcomes, and credible peer references can reveal the real segment.

**Apply it:** Compare your best customers' situations and test whether their testimonials persuade prospective buyers with similar needs. Use the resulting distinctions in product choices, messaging, and qualification.

**Boundary:** Geography, regulation, and company size still belong in the definition when they materially determine use or purchase. A famous customer logo alone does not justify moving upmarket; the article's dimensions table is a set of prompts, not a checklist to fill exhaustively.
