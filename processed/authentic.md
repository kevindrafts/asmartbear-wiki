# Human + Fallible = Love; Corporate + Sterile = Refund

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/authentic/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen connects forgiveness to an ongoing relationship with recognizable people. A sincere apology helps when customers have already seen care, competence, and improvements after mistakes. He suggests discovering a company voice by examining what communication you admire or reject, then making clear choices.

**Apply it:** Make everyday support and public communication consistent with the behavior you promise during a failure. Explain the remedy and what prevents recurrence.

**Boundary:** Personality alienates some buyers as it attracts others. An apology is not a substitute for fixing negligent service, and stylistic influence does not justify copying another company’s work.
