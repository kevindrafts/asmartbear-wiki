# Change: Damned if you do, damned more if you don’t

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/change/) · Unofficial editorial note.

People can want a better outcome while resisting the disruption required to achieve it. Cohen argues that adaptation remains necessary but that repeated change consumes emotional capacity, even among enthusiastic employees. Some attempted improvements also make matters worse.

**Apply it:** Explain why a change follows from new evidence, watch for fatigue, and allow recovery while continuing to learn.

**Boundary:** Constant motion is not proof of good leadership. The article distinguishes thoughtful adaptation from an organization with no coherent reasoning, and kindness does not remove the need to judge whether changes help.
