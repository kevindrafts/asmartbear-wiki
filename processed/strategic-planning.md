# Quarterly strategic planning using the fairytale structure

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/strategic-planning/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen uses a narrative structure to connect near-term work with a longer-term strategy. A useful plan identifies desired change, the obstacles preventing it, chosen action, evidence of progress, and important work intentionally deferred. He emphasizes exploring the problem before selecting solutions and distinguishing meaningful outcomes from a high volume of completed tasks.

**Apply it:** Write a short explanation of why the selected work matters now, including the constraint it removes and what observers should see change. Record difficult exclusions so teams can defend focus under pressure.

**Boundary:** The framework organizes judgment rather than supplying great ideas. Qualitative outcomes can matter when stated clearly. The Tesla section is the author's illustrative reconstruction, not a verified internal plan or a license to defer mandatory safety requirements.
