# Mission, Vision, poTAYto, poTAHto

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/mission-vision/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen separates three jobs often blurred by mission language: the positive change beyond the company, a concrete future state for the company, and the next observable milestone. A vision identifies what will be built, for whom, and the distinguishing insight; a milestone works backward into action. Purpose may motivate the company from inception or emerge from the good its work creates.

**Apply it:** Write all three separately and connect the next milestone to a real strategic obstacle.

**Boundary:** The essay explicitly notes weak evidence that purpose alone improves financial performance. A purpose must influence behavior; elegant language and broad aspirations do not substitute for execution.
