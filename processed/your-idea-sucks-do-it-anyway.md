# Your idea sucks, now go do it anyway

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/your-idea-sucks-do-it-anyway/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Historical accounts of PayPal, Flickr, and Smart Bear illustrate businesses discovering a stronger direction after an imperfect starting concept. Cohen argues that trying to perfect the initial idea privately can postpone the experiences needed to change it. Smart Bear's several early tools eventually gave way to the code-review product that customers valued.

**Apply it:** Make a bounded attempt that exposes the concept to real use and buying behavior, and treat unexpected demand as information about the next direction.

**Boundary:** Successful pivots are selected examples, not evidence that every weak idea will improve. This encouragement to begin belongs alongside the corpus's advice to test existential risks and stop when continued investment lacks a credible case.
