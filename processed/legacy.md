# Legacy

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/legacy/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen considers legacy as the continuing effect of ideas rather than the persistence of the creator's name. His metaphor follows ripples that influence other people even after their origin is forgotten. He connects this to useful writing and to the values of a deceased parent continuing through a child's actions.

**Apply it:** Ask which ideas or behaviors are worth helping others carry forward, beyond attracting attention to yourself.

**Boundary:** Cohen acknowledges that his financial security makes detachment from fame easier. He explicitly preserves the value of attribution and payment and condemns plagiarism; the reflection is not permission to copy his work.
