# When you want to quit because it’s just not worth it

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startups-emotionally-draining/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen recounts a procurement negotiation that left him feeling inadequate despite an established, growing business. The buyer's threat eventually proved weaker than it sounded, but factual resolution did not immediately remove its emotional effect. His point is that founders can continue useful work without becoming permanently confident or emotionally invulnerable.

**Apply it:** Separate a difficult interaction's commercial facts from the larger story it prompts about your competence, and seek perspective from trusted peers and actual decision-makers.

**Boundary:** One hostile negotiation does not characterize every procurement team. Persistence alone does not establish a viable venture or require enduring burnout; the page links a later framework that revisits coping with these feelings.
