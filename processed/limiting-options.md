# Limiting Options

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/limiting-options/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen uses Othello's attention to an opponent's available moves as a competitive-strategy analogy. Exclusive relationships, scarce distribution, and a strongly established expert reputation can force competitors toward harder or less attractive alternatives. His Smart Bear example ties authority in code review to what enterprise buyers valued.

**Apply it:** Identify the response a competitor would prefer, then ask whether your customer-valued position makes that response difficult to reproduce.

**Boundary:** The game analogy does not make business a closed, zero-sum system. The article's historical claims about solved games and broad dismissal of software patents are not relied on here; exclusivity and spending still require independent economic and legal evaluation.
