# Customers are cancelling. Help!

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customers-are-cancelling/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer treats cancellations as evidence about the gap between the promised and experienced value. Generic explanations such as price may conceal workflow or fit problems, and former customers can be difficult to reach.

**Apply it:** Interview departing customers, look for earlier signs of unsuccessful use, and distinguish ideal-customer feedback from mismatched demand.

**Boundary:** Usage patterns are clues for investigation, not automatic causal explanations. More features or polish will not necessarily repair an expectation or customer-selection problem.
