# The mid-market briar patch

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/mid-market/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Mid-sized buyers can combine enterprise-style purchasing complexity with limited budgets. Cohen warns that headcount alone does not define a coherent segment: industry, workflow, and problem can matter much more. The business needs enough revenue and service margin to support its selling and delivery effort.

**Apply it:** Model a segment's buying process and ongoing support demands against attainable contract value, rather than assuming an unoccupied middle is attractive.

**Boundary:** This is a caution, not a prohibition. A later note explains that WP Engine entered larger segments after it could fund the necessary sales, service, compliance, and account-management capabilities.
