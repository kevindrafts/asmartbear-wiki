# Idiot! Buying SmartBear.com

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/idiot-buying-smartbear-com/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen recounts postponing a modest domain purchase, then spending substantially more to recover the company-name address from another owner. A substitute domain created recurring explanation and typing friction. His resistance eventually became about fairness and principle rather than the business's practical needs.

**Apply it:** Compare a small preventive expense with the repeated operational friction of deferring it; reassess a dispute using present costs rather than resentment.

**Boundary:** This is one historical domain-purchase experience. The incidental statements about entities, taxes, and trademarks are not current legal or tax guidance.
