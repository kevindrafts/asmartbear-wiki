# Out of the cesspool and into the sewer: A/B testing trap

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/local-minimum/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Small improvements can optimize the wrong starting point. Cohen's water-flow analogy shows how following only nearby gains leaves a search trapped while a better region remains unexplored. He recommends testing substantially different messages, layouts, or customer propositions alongside incremental variants.

**Apply it:** Reserve experimentation capacity for a distinct hypothesis about why customers buy, and use results to reconsider the broader positioning when appropriate.

**Boundary:** A bold variant is an experiment, not a presumed improvement. The essay still calls for statistical discipline; tiny fluctuations should not be mistaken for either progress or proof that optimization is exhausted.
