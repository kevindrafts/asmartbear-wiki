# Which is better: Many customers at low price-point or few at high price?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/price-vs-quantity/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Equal revenue can conceal very different businesses: many small accounts demonstrate broad acquisition, while a few expensive accounts may better support a small owner-operated company. Cohen examines market size, selling risk, learning opportunities, support costs, and financing goals rather than declaring one model universally superior. Paying customers provide different evidence from a large free-user population.

**Apply it:** Compare the acquisition and service system each price requires against the founder's desired company size and capital needs.

**Boundary:** The essay assumes some economics can improve over time, but explicitly rejects relying on implausibly large improvements. A thousand current customers is evidence of reach, not a guarantee of ten thousand more.
