# Stop chopping yourself to pieces

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/stop-chopping-to-pieces/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen criticizes a fragmented workday in which messages and interruptions dictate attention. He connects concentrated work and regular recovery with both satisfaction and effectiveness, asking readers to compare those preferences with their actual schedules. The essay's pressure comes from the gap between knowing a useful practice and arranging time to follow it.

**Apply it:** Create a protected block for one task, batch interruptible work where the role allows it, and make room for recovery.

**Boundary:** This is a brief exhortation rather than a documented research review or medical prescription. Individual control over time varies with caregiving, support coverage, and other responsibilities.
