# The true meaning of common idioms

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/idioms/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen's satirical glossary translates familiar business expressions into the evasions, uncertainty, and self-interest they can conceal. Inflated positioning, vague promises, and polite disagreement all receive deliberately cynical interpretations. Later reader contributions expand the original collection.

**Apply it:** Use the joke as a prompt to replace an ambiguous stock phrase with the actual decision, evidence, or commitment.

**Boundary:** These are humorous alternative readings, not literal definitions or reliable evidence of a speaker's hidden intent. The financial jokes should not be mistaken for accounting rules.
