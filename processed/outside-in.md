# Outside-In

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/outside-in/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Service claims become credible when prospects can experience them. Cohen describes inviting trial users to test support directly, turning an otherwise generic promise into something observable. The willingness to be tested can itself signal confidence and openness.

**Apply it:** Give a prospect a straightforward way to evaluate the behavior behind an important promise, rather than adding more adjectives to the claim.

**Boundary:** The opening examples are rhetorical, not a literal rule that stated virtues are always false. Demonstration must be backed by consistent delivery after the sale.
