# Never say “no,” but rarely say “yes.”

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/say-yes/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen reconciles focus with opportunity by setting terms under which an apparent distraction would materially support the core business. Examples include pricing exceptional support demands to fund capacity and charging for travel time that displaced product work. A refusal then protects focus, while acceptance provides resources sufficient to justify the diversion.

**Apply it:** Before quoting unusual work, define the additional capacity or strategic project it must fund, including delivery risk and displaced work. Make the offer only if you can honor it.

**Boundary:** A large price does not solve an impossible commitment or every strategic mismatch. WP Engine's target segment changed as capabilities grew; the article's early limits and later update should not be collapsed into one timeless customer policy.
