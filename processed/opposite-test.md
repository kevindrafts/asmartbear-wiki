# The “Opposite Test”

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/opposite-test/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A claim says little about strategy if its opposite is obviously absurd. Cohen uses this test to replace generic virtues with choices a sensible competitor might decline: broad integration versus depth, or compatibility versus freedom to redesign. A real choice usually has an acknowledged disadvantage.

**Apply it:** Reverse each major positioning claim and ask who would rationally choose that alternative. Make the resulting tradeoff explicit.

**Boundary:** A generic quality can still define a position when the product is demonstrably exceptional on that dimension. The test screens for meaningful choices; it does not prove customers care about the chosen difference.
