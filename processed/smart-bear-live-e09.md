# E09 w/ Jon Sturgeon: AI changed his agency's model. Should he grow or sell?

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/9/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/9) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Jon Sturgeon, Mario Peshev.

Mario Peshev considers the future of DevriX after changing its delivery model with AI. Cohen and Jon Sturgeon examine operations, selling, and possible exits. Their advice sometimes differs; the discussion is useful as competing diagnoses, not a single endorsed playbook.

- **Distinguish waste from paid readiness.** Sturgeon presses for higher utilization. Cohen observes that keeping people available for variable demand can be a valid service if the price covers that reserve. Peshev questions whether his current pricing does so. This does not establish a universal utilization target or justify treating all spare capacity alike. [56:04–57:13](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/9/transcript#t=56m4s).
- **Build a sales system beyond the founder.** Cohen distinguishes persuasive personal selling from designing and managing a repeatable sales process. He questions the use of marketers whose client work competes with growing the agency; Sturgeon emphasizes hiring capable salespeople. The suggested staffing is specific to the conversation, not a general formula. [1:12:12–1:14:20](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/9/transcript#t=1h12m12s).
- **Clarify the future before choosing the transaction.** Sturgeon argues for reducing dependence on the founder even if no sale occurs. Cohen separates improving and selling an operating agency from finding a strategic buyer for its AI-enabled capability. He explicitly says he does not know which buyers are seeking that capability. [1:37:15–1:39:47](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/9/transcript#t=1h37m15s); [buyer hypothesis](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/9/transcript#t=1h42m59s).

**Apply it:** Describe what customers are paying you to keep available, which growth work has protected ownership, and which future job you want as founder. Evaluate the proposed changes against those three answers. This is editorial synthesis.

**Boundary:** Reported productivity gains and financial figures are not independently audited. Buyer stories, sale prices, and timelines are speculative; no acquisition or subsequent operating result is demonstrated. Do not attribute Sturgeon's staffing or outbound tactics wholesale to Cohen.
