# A compass is not a map

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/compass/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen compares startup frameworks with navigational aids that cannot choose a destination or guarantee a route. Successful and unsuccessful companies can both follow a practice—or reject it—so a favorite founder’s story does not establish a law.

**Apply it:** Use advice to generate and examine options within your circumstances, rather than as authority that settles a decision.

**Boundary:** The essay does not make experience useless. It cautions against survivor bias and predicting an individual venture from a selected success story, including Cohen’s own frameworks.
