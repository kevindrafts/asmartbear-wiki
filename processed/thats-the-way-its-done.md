# “Because that’s the way it’s done” — should a founder listen?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/thats-the-way-its-done/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Smart Bear sold to large companies despite a playful name, informal voice, and unconventional sales process. Cohen does not conclude that established methods are wrong; he argues that a workable alternative may better fit the founder and become a distinction. Departing from convention also creates problems that conventional advice may not solve.

**Apply it:** Separate an actual customer requirement from an industry habit, and test an alternative that fits your strengths while acknowledging its costs.

**Boundary:** A successful exception establishes possibility, not a universal replacement. Being different has value only when it serves the business and its customers; discomfort with a normal practice alone does not validate the alternative.
