# E03 w/ Craig Hewitt: Restarting growth after stuck at €16k/mo

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/3/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/3) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Craig Hewitt, Tiago Ferreira, João Amaro.

Podsqueeze's founders describe a long plateau despite more features and substantial inbound traffic. Cohen and Hewitt examine whether the recurring business depends on customers whose need disappears, then consider an agency segment that the founders report retaining better.

- **Understand why the need stops.** Hewitt distinguishes podcast hosting, which can remain useful after publishing stops, from production tools whose use ends with the show. More traffic alone does not repair that mismatch. The founders' agency customers suggest a different pattern of ongoing work, though broader success is still unproven. [13:43–15:25](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/3/transcript#t=13m43s).
- **Expansion needs a value measure that can grow.** Hewitt notes that a successful show need not publish more minutes. Cohen asks what customers would willingly pay more for as their commitment deepens, rather than simply attaching higher charges to their success. The proposed packaging still needs customer research. [1:03:17–1:07:58](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/3/transcript#t=1h3m17s).
- **Investigate the actual loss.** Cohen separates prospects who buy a competitor from those who do nothing. Hewitt raises another possibility: partial automation may leave enough editing and publishing work that switching is unattractive. Neither explanation is established for every lost deal. [1:24:18–1:28:40](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/3/transcript#t=1h24m18s).
- **Test the positioning against successful users.** An integrated workflow could be valuable, but Cohen explicitly withdraws that pitch if customers mainly value one component. Interview their retained customers before leading with an all-in-one claim. [1:46:30–1:49:14](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/3/transcript#t=1h46m30s).

**Apply it:** Compare retained and departing cohorts by ongoing job, buying trigger, and actual alternative. Use the strongest contrast to choose an interview or positioning test. This sequence is editorial synthesis.

**Boundary:** The conversation does not prove that agencies will solve growth or that self-service should be shut down. Cohen discusses preserving current income while testing a new segment. Business figures, acquisition values, and industry estimates are conversational claims, not audited benchmarks; transcript wording has not been checked against audio.
