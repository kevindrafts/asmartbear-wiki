# Sacrifice your health for your startup

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/sacrifice-your-health-for-your-startup/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This controversial 2009 essay presents Cohen's experience of bootstrapping as consuming nearly all available attention, even away from work. He argues that productivity improvements do not eliminate an unlimited backlog and asks prospective founders to recognize the personal cost before committing. A later editor's note retains that account while distinguishing it from subsequent hustle-culture interpretations.

**Apply it:** Make the intended workload, household expectations, runway, and limits explicit before starting, rather than treating personal costs as an unexpected surprise.

**Boundary:** Successful founders' anecdotes do not establish that harming health or relationships causes success or is necessary. This historical perspective is not health advice or a prescription to ignore illness; compare it with Cohen's writing on burnout, emotional strain, and sustainable work.
