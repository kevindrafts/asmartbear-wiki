# Your non-linear problem of 90% utilization

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/utilization/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen uses server capacity to explain why filling every person's schedule can reduce throughput. Normal variation and interruptions consume spare capacity; when all colleagues are near their limits, one disruption propagates through dependencies. Apparent efficiency becomes delays, context switching, and brittle delivery.

**Apply it:** Plan capacity for ordinary variation, review cross-team dependencies, and distinguish temporary, purposeful surges from a permanent overloaded baseline.

**Boundary:** The percentages illustrate a queueing intuition rather than a measured universal target for human work. Spare capacity should be sized to the role's variability and responsibilities, and occasional slack is not evidence that a person contributes too little.
