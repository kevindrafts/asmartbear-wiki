# Invention is Drudgery

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/drudgery/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Finished achievements hide the repetitive work, failed attempts, and unglamorous preparation behind them. Cohen uses invention and performance to reframe the frustration of being partway through a difficult project.

**Apply it:** Budget for iteration and operational detail rather than expecting continuous creative excitement.

**Boundary:** Exhaustion does not prove progress, and the essay acknowledges that quitting versus continuing can be unclear. Its historical examples illustrate persistence, not a guarantee that enough effort produces success.
