# Darwin doesn’t automatically select the best companies

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/darwinian-company-growth/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Market survival selects for propagation, Cohen argues, rather than automatically selecting ethical behavior, employee welfare, or even a sustainable business model. He asks founders to make values part of how growth happens instead of treating growth as sufficient justification.

**Apply it:** Name outcomes for customers, employees, and society that financial success must support.

**Boundary:** The biological analogy is not a causal model of markets. The company profitability examples reflect the article’s historical context, and Cohen accepts that growth can be necessary in some markets.
