# Why it’s nice to compete against a large, profitable company

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/compete-on-profit/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A profitable incumbent validates demand but may resist price reductions that damage its earnings. Cohen argues that a startup with a genuinely different cost structure can exploit that constraint. His margin example shows how a modest revenue reduction can erase a much larger fraction of profit.

**Apply it:** Identify the incumbent’s actual profit center and your structural cost advantage before competing on price.

**Boundary:** Attacking a subsidized loss leader reverses the logic: the incumbent may willingly spend without needing that product to earn money. Historical company examples should not be read as current segment financials.
