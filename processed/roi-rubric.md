# Fermi ROI: Fixing the ROI rubric

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/roi-rubric/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Small differences in an ROI spreadsheet can be overwhelmed by errors in both benefit and effort. Cohen proposes broad, explicitly defined scoring bands so debate focuses on meaningful differences in assumptions. His worked tables separate prerequisites for one outcome from unrelated objectives: multiplication fits the former conceptual model, while unrelated benefits need a priority order or an explicit trade-off. Ties become honest uncertainty rather than defects to hide with extra decimals.

**Apply it:** Define what each score means, compare decisions under plausible estimation error, and explain the winner in ordinary language. Break genuine ties using a declared secondary aim such as team enthusiasm.

**Boundary:** Coarse scoring does not make guesses accurate or guarantee robust rankings. This is a decision aid; strategic commitments and tiny maintenance tasks need different treatment, and numerical weights encode judgment rather than objective value.
