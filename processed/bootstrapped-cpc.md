# Bootstrapped CPC rule of thumb: ARPU/25

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/bootstrapped-cpc/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen derives an early advertising bid heuristic from assumed customer lifetime, acquisition-cost discipline, and visitor-to-purchase conversion. Under his stated assumptions, the result is monthly average revenue per user divided by 25. The image-based general formula is CPC = ARPU × r / (5c), with conversion r and monthly cancellation c.

**Apply it:** Substitute your actual channel conversion, retention, service costs, and cash constraints before using the result to set bids. Include indirect acquisition costs.

**Boundary:** This is a pre-data heuristic for a profit-oriented bootstrapper. The simplified lifetime calculation omits gross margin, explicitly acknowledged in a footnote, and expected lifetime can be unreliable for young cohorts. Long-run returns do not remove a cash-flow constraint.
