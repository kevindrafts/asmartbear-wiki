# Procrastinate for Success!

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/procrastinate/) · Unofficial editorial note.

An overwhelmed founder cannot complete every plausible task. Cohen argues that deferring lower-value work lets some apparent urgencies resolve themselves and preserves time for the few activities that determine success. Batching routine work reduces switching costs; enjoyable work can also help sustain energy.

**Apply it:** Distinguish a task's immediate pressure from its actual consequence, and batch work whose timing permits it.

**Boundary:** The essay intentionally rationalizes procrastination and includes provocative accounting examples. It does not establish that penalties, missed obligations, or ignored customer problems are harmless; delay needs an explicit assessment of consequences.
