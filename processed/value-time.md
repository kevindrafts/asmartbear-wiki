# What is the value of one hour of a startup founder’s time?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/value-time/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that uncertain, deferred startup rewards require a higher bar for founder time than a conventional consulting rate. His hypothetical payment gamble reframes delegation and prioritization: focus on work where personal strengths meet consequential business needs, rather than spending scarce attention on every task that can be done cheaply in-house.

**Apply it:** Compare the opportunity cost of a task with the cost and feasibility of delegating it, while protecting the work most likely to change the venture's prospects.

**Boundary:** The headline hourly figure is an illustrative hurdle, not a market valuation or promised return. Cash constraints still matter, and the essay's call to overwork should be read alongside its broader discussion of capacity and sustainable productivity.
