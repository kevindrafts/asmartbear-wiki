# E05 w/ Laura Roeder: Scared to commit to his best customers

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/5/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/5) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Laura Roeder, Hasan Cagli.

Hasan Cagli is finding better customers for PostPlanify among agencies, but hesitates to narrow the product further. Cohen and Roeder turn the broad agency label into choices about clients, workflow, product scope, and pricing.

- **Define the work, not just the category.** The discussion distinguishes an agency doing social-media work for clients from a service in which clients also author and manage content. Those choices imply different permissions, onboarding, and support burdens. Cagli ultimately favors the agency doing the work with limited client approval. [Workflow discussion](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/5/transcript#t=1h25m35s).
- **Make a choice that produces evidence.** Roeder argues that further deliberation cannot remove uncertainty about a niche. Cohen also pushes for a choice grounded in the strongest existing customers. Together, these suggest a testable commitment rather than either indefinite indecision or certainty about the market. [42:06](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/5/transcript#t=0h42m6s).
- **Connect functionality to the buyer's reason for wanting it.** Reflecting on MeetEdgar, Roeder distinguishes a recurring-content feature from understanding which buyer wants that workflow and why. A feature-based niche still needs a customer and outcome. [1:07:13](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/5/transcript#t=1h7m13s).
- **Choose a charging unit customers understand.** They explore pricing around agency clients and workspaces. Cohen's heuristic is to let the bill rise with the customer's business success, while explicitly rejecting a universal pricing law. [1:42:26](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/5/transcript#t=1h42m26s).

**Apply it:** Describe who creates, approves, and publishes the work for your best customers. Use that description to choose one workflow and pricing hypothesis to test. This is an editorial application, not a finalized plan from the episode.

**Boundary:** Suggested niches, features, and prices are proposals, not demonstrated results. The official transcript contains some inconsistent speaker labels and timestamp ordering; this note uses clear exchanges and joint attribution where appropriate. No audio verification has been performed.
