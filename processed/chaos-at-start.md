# It’s a torturous chaos until it isn’t

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/chaos-at-start/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen looks behind later growth charts to the slow, uncertain beginnings of successful companies. Those early periods can resemble ventures that never work. The charts’ main lesson is how a later scale can hide the difficult years before demand accelerated.

**Apply it:** Avoid treating distress or slow progress alone as decisive evidence of inevitable failure. Continue examining customers and the business itself.

**Boundary:** The same experience can precede failure. Cohen explicitly says the eventual outcome may be impossible to distinguish at the time; the essay does not recommend endless persistence.
