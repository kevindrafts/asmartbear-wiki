# When should a decision be fast, or slow?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/decisions-fast-slow/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Speed usually accelerates learning, but Cohen identifies reasons to deliberate: irreversibility, large commitments, weak evaluation methods, unfamiliar decisions, and delayed feedback. Waiting is useful when it produces relevant information; otherwise it may only postpone action.

**Apply it:** Identify the source of decision risk and redesign the choice to reduce it—for example through a reversible, limited commitment.

**Boundary:** Despite calling the prompts a scorecard, Cohen explicitly rejects scoring and summing them. Sometimes the best decision is to defer an unnecessary decision altogether.
