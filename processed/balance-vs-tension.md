# Balance is weak. Tension is strong.

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/balance-vs-tension/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen distinguishes productive disagreement from compromise that removes everything distinctive. His physical analogies support a managerial preference for keeping strong perspectives in active tension rather than diluting each until nobody objects.

**Apply it:** Preserve opposing expertise in a team decision and ask how both perspectives can improve the result. Maintain respect while allowing substantive disagreement.

**Boundary:** This is an analogy about team behavior, not a mechanical law of organizations. Cohen explicitly recognizes that extremes can become reckless; tension needs coordination and does not excuse hostility.
