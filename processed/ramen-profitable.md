# Profitable on day one!

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ramen-profitable/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A product covering its hosting bill may still depend on an unpaid founder and shrinking personal savings. Cohen proposes progressively stronger viability tests: supporting basic living costs, replacing prior earnings, and funding the people needed to operate without the founder's unpaid labor. His own companies took years to reach stronger versions of that standard.

**Apply it:** Report operating cash surplus separately from founder compensation, personal runway, and replacement labor. Make the next sustainability milestone explicit instead of celebrating an ambiguous profit label.

**Boundary:** These are practical owner-economics tests, not formal accounting definitions. Early subsidy is not proof of failure, and a founder's previous salary or lifestyle is not a universal benchmark for every business.
