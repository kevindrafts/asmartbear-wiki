# The “Talk vs Walk” workshop

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/talk-vs-walk/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen's exercise compares the prominence of a company's claims with how well the product delivers them. Independent idea generation precedes discussion so the chart includes quiet strengths as well as conspicuous promises. Disagreement often reveals missing information, inconsistent definitions, or different customer segments. The resulting actions can change messaging, delivery, or the decision to compete on a dimension.

**Apply it:** Bring product, sales, support, and marketing evidence to a claim-by-claim review. Distinguish meaningful differentiation from shared table stakes, and assign action where customers experience a mismatch.

**Boundary:** The axes express judgments, not measured coordinates. Split materially different audiences instead of averaging away a failure, and correct misleading claims rather than treating exaggeration as a growth tactic.
