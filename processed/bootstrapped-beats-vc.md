# Bootstrapped beats VC?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/bootstrapped-beats-vc/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A bootstrapped company can compete by choosing customers and activities for which a funded rival’s scale is less useful. Cohen points to niches too small to interest a large competitor, founder-level service, distinctive personality, and focused products with deliberately different trade-offs. He also suggests attacking incumbent profit centers with a different cost structure.

**Apply it:** Name the specific buyer who values your strengths, then choose a channel where money alone does not determine access. Personal support and useful content can reinforce that choice.

**Boundary:** This is an argument for selecting the contest carefully, not proof that small companies always move faster or win. A narrower opportunity may be an excellent owner-operated business while being unsuitable for venture return expectations.
