# COC: A new metric for measuring cancellations in SaaS business models

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/coc/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen expresses the burden of replacing cancelled revenue as COC = p × c: acquisition cost per dollar of monthly revenue multiplied by monthly revenue loss rate. The framing makes churn comparable with other operating costs and shows why cheaper acquisition or higher account revenue can matter alongside retention.

**Apply it:** Estimate replacement spending, then examine acquisition efficiency, pricing, and expansion opportunities together.

**Boundary:** The initial p uses a simplified payback measure excluding gross margin. The article later refines c to net revenue churn, including downgrades and upgrades. Zero net churn removes this replacement burden, not the need to fund service or growth.
