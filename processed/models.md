# All pretty models are wrong, but some ugly models are useful

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/models/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A model can help predict or organize something without fully explaining it. Cohen uses that distinction to question business frameworks designed for attractive symmetry rather than messy organizations. He favors models that expose interacting forces, uneven categories, uncertainty, and limits of applicability. The diagrams contrast neat classifications with activity maps and less uniform structures.

**Apply it:** Ask what evidence supports a framework, which real-world complications it leaves out, and what decisions it improves.

**Boundary:** Visual asymmetry is not proof of validity, just Cohen's screening heuristic. The physics and historical examples are explanatory analogies; business frameworks lack the controlled testing those examples invoke.
