# Product/Market Fit (PMF): Experience & Data

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-market-fit/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen identifies fit through sustained customer pull, meaningful adoption, and retention together. A busy sales funnel alone is insufficient if customers quickly leave. His company charts depict a change to a steeper linear growth trajectory, rather than automatic exponential growth. WP Engine’s support overload illustrates how success can require an abrupt operating change.

**Apply it:** Examine acquisition and retention together, and prepare delivery capacity when demand changes. Distinguish customer losses from their increasing absolute revenue impact as the installed base grows.

**Boundary:** The article proposes specific revenue, usage, and cancellation thresholds from Cohen’s experience; these are his definition, not a universal standard. Buffer and SparkToro illustrate retention constraints, but a growth plateau can coexist with a worthwhile profitable company. The charts are selected examples, not independent causal proof.
