# Look what we did

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/proud-of-founding/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen's tree metaphor gives a founder a necessary historical role without attributing the whole organization to that person. Visible achievements and unseen operational work are sustained by many contributors. He extends the metaphor to employees carrying skills, opportunities, and values into later chapters of their lives.

**Apply it:** Evaluate the company partly by whether people grow, do work they respect, and leave better equipped to contribute elsewhere.

**Boundary:** This is a personal account of purpose and shared credit, not a claim that every employee has the same experience. Source headcounts are explicitly updated historical context.
