# Am I cut out to be a founder?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/am-i-founder/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag response treats founder suitability as alignment between motivations, strengths, market needs, and the daily realities of entrepreneurship. Willingness to work hard does not answer what kind of company or life you want.

**Apply it:** Describe the outcome you seek, the particular advantage you bring, and the rejection and uncertainty you are prepared to face. Compare the daily work with your long-term aims.

**Boundary:** Introspection cannot guarantee business success. Passion needs a plausible market, and choosing a sustainable lifestyle business can reflect different priorities from pursuing maximum scale.
