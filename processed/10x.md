# Laws of 10x found everywhere

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/10x/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen collects examples of highly uneven returns across people, products, channels, customers, and decisions. His practical claim is that searching for the dominant contributor can matter more than refining many minor variables. He also points out that disproportionate support demand and unprofitable accounts deserve investigation.

**Apply it:** Look for the channel, decision, or product attribute that contributes most to the outcome you need. Measure concentration before distributing attention evenly.

**Boundary:** The article treats order-of-magnitude differences as a heuristic, not a universal law. A costly customer is not automatically someone to dismiss, and an opportunity that once dominated may eventually be exhausted. The examples suggest where to investigate; they do not justify assuming a particular distribution without evidence.
