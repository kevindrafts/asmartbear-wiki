# Telling the 800-lb Gorilla to Shove it up his Ass

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/competing-big-companies/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen recommends seeking a viable position rather than defining success as destroying a giant. Small companies may move into narrower markets, serve neglected needs, or react faster. His Smart Bear example describes an incumbent validating a category without fully serving its users.

**Apply it:** Name the customer need a competitor’s existing commitments make awkward to address. Watch other focused startups as well as large firms.

**Boundary:** The examples reflect their publication context. Incumbents can adapt, and the later AI essay specifically challenges several assumed startup advantages; size alone is not protection.
