# Lost confidence

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/confidence/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen challenges numerical confidence scores that are undefined and uncalibrated yet heavily influence priority. He proposes reliable improvements, observed behavior, bounded experiments, and investments whose potential benefit justifies survivable losses. The confidence-versus-results table emphasizes that conviction alone predicts poorly.

**Apply it:** State a spending or time boundary before a risky project, and distinguish reliable incremental work from attempts at major differentiation.

**Boundary:** Optionality itself costs effort and may be inappropriate early. The article acknowledges limitations of its portfolio mathematics under startup-like return distributions; neither diversification nor an asymmetric-looking idea guarantees an outcome.
