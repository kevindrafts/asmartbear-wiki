# Refutation: An acquisition is always a failure

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/acquisition-failure/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen rejects judging every acquisition as a failure of ambition. Independence does not preserve an early team’s culture indefinitely either, and founders’ dissatisfaction after a sale need not describe employees’ experience. An acquirer can provide investment, stability, and new opportunities.

**Apply it:** Compare plausible futures for customers, employees, and founders separately, including the risks of remaining independent.

**Boundary:** Some acquisitions destroy products and jobs. The argument challenges a blanket verdict; it does not establish that selling is generally preferable or that any named historical deal benefited everyone.
