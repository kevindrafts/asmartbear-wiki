# I need higher-quality marketing leads

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/higher-quality-marketing-leads/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer treats poor affiliate traffic as a reason to examine channel fit and the wider acquisition system. Cohen connects customer location and founder strengths with positioning, pricing, available channel inventory, and website conversion. A landing-page improvement can benefit several channels at once.

**Apply it:** Separate a channel's quality problem from its volume ceiling, then run a bounded alternative-channel experiment while improving the shared conversion path.

**Boundary:** A price increase or new channel is a hypothesis, not a guaranteed doubling. The answer outlines a transition strategy rather than supplying a budget, timetable, or measured forecast.
