# Ho Rudolph

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ho-rudolph/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This fictional exchange reverses the familiar Rudolph story: the previously excluded reindeer refuses an urgent request to rescue the group. The humor comes from treating sudden usefulness as an inadequate substitute for years of respect and inclusion.

**Apply it:** As an editorial reading of the satire, consider whether a request relies on goodwill that earlier behavior has depleted.

**Boundary:** This is comic fiction, not an empirical management framework. Its exaggerated voice and refusal are part of the joke, not a script for handling workplace conflict.
