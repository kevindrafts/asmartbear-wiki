# Our unhealthy fixation with emulating #1

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/copy-number-one/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A single-elimination tournament produces an undefeated winner even when every match is random. Cohen uses that diagram to question how much can be inferred from a market leader’s rank. Skill, luck, and the selection system are entangled.

**Apply it:** Seek relevant companies whose circumstances and choices illuminate your own, including successful firms outside the spotlight.

**Boundary:** The analogy does not claim all winners are lucky or all practices equally useful. It challenges copying a famous company without knowing which choices mattered or whether its stage matches yours.
