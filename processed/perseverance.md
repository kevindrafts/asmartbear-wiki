# Stubborn Visionaries & Pigheaded Fools

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/perseverance/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Before the outcome is known, wise persistence and costly stubbornness can look identical. Cohen warns against turning hindsight into a simple rule to quit or continue. His reader-contributed questions examine progress, remaining ideas, energy, opportunity cost, differentiation, and the original reason for pursuing the work.

**Apply it:** Set a bounded period for the next attempt, specify evidence of improvement, and use an outside sounding board to challenge rationalizations.

**Boundary:** The questions support judgment rather than guarantee the right answer. Persistence should not be decided solely by sunk effort, and another company's success establishes possibility rather than your own probability of success.
