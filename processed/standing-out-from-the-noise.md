# Standing out from the noise

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/standing-out-from-the-noise/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen uses music tested on poor speakers as an analogy for marketing encountered amid distractions. A pitch polished for an attentive audience may fail when someone catches only fragments. The competition includes messages, interruptions, and the option to leave, not just rival products.

**Apply it:** Test whether the main idea survives a brief, interrupted viewing or hearing. Ask someone unfamiliar with the product what they understood, and repeat a coherent message across relevant encounters.

**Boundary:** Noise testing is a communication exercise rather than proof of demand. Repetition cannot repair an irrelevant promise, and the essay's media examples belong to its historical setting.
