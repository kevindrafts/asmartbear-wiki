# The full story of “the one important thing” for startups

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/one-priority/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A conversation with Noah Kagan pushed Cohen to ask which change would transform the business rather than merely improve a ratio. At early WP Engine, a major increase in signups proved more valuable than the modest retention improvement initially under discussion. Concentrating on one outcome helped generate larger interventions.

**Apply it:** Identify the metric whose material improvement would change the company's prospects, then compare initiatives by their ability to produce that change.

**Boundary:** This is a specific early-stage episode, not advice to ignore severe churn indefinitely. Cohen's later growth-plateau framework gives retention priority when losses become the binding constraint.
