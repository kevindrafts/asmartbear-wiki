# When being an “expert” is harmful

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/expert-harmful/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Experience becomes a liability when it prevents testing assumptions. Cohen describes founders who inferred prices and buying behavior from earlier industry knowledge without asking current customers. Even selling a new product to familiar buyers can introduce different budgets, constraints, and workflows.

**Apply it:** Use expertise and contacts to form stronger hypotheses and obtain interviews, then actively seek contradictions.

**Boundary:** Experience can be a real advantage when paired with curiosity. The historical advertising comparison illustrates changing channels, not a current performance benchmark.
