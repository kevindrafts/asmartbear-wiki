# Easy to criticize, hard to create

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/criticize-create/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen distinguishes predicting failure from helping create something worthwhile. Since ventures often fail, blanket skepticism can look accurate without being useful. Constructive entrepreneurship requires both conviction and the ability to revise a mistaken belief.

**Apply it:** Separate a critic’s tone from any actionable truth and ask what evidence would change your position.

**Boundary:** Ignoring all criticism is as easy and dangerous as criticizing everything. The Netflix example concerns the earlier DVD business; it is not a current analysis of streaming economics.
