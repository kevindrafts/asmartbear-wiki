# For probabilities, use Fermi numbers, not words

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/probability-words/) · Unofficial editorial note.

People attach very different numerical meanings to words such as likely and improbable. Cohen's plots show variation between people and inconsistencies even within one person's vocabulary. He recommends a small set of explicit probability estimates so disagreement becomes visible without pretending to know precise decimals.

**Apply it:** Elicit coarse estimates independently, discuss large differences, and state the evidence and context behind them.

**Boundary:** Numerical labels improve communication, not necessarily calibration. The essay acknowledges that probabilities may be inappropriate or unknowable for some judgments; a chosen number should not disguise that uncertainty.
