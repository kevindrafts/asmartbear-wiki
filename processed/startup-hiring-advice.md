# Hiring Employee #1

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-hiring-advice/) · Unofficial editorial note.

For a very early hire, Cohen emphasizes comfort with ambiguity, clear communication, and the ability to represent the company to customers. He suggests using a distinctive job description, brief written responses, and deeper discussion of actual experience instead of treating résumé keywords as proof. Recruiting relationships should begin before the capacity shortage becomes urgent.

**Apply it:** Describe the real working conditions and assess relevant work and communication consistently. Build a candidate network while estimating the delay between deciding to hire and gaining productive capacity.

**Boundary:** This is advice for the first few employees, not every stage. Its preference for personal chemistry and sweeping recruiter criticism need caution: shared humor is not job competence, and enthusiasm for chaos should not replace fair evaluation, clear expectations, or lawful employment practices.
