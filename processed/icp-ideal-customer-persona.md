# Selling to Carol: Why targeting an ICP brings 10x more customers than you expected

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/icp-ideal-customer-persona/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen’s ideal customer, Carol, is defined through buying conditions and product fit rather than invented biographical details. Specific messaging helps this person recognize the product; adjacent buyers can still choose the same trade-offs. Subaru’s positioning illustrates how a clear target need not describe every eventual purchaser.

**Apply it:** Combine sales wins, support experience, retention data, and interviews to describe who urgently values your advantages. Segment activation and cancellations by that fit. A large account can be a costly distraction if its requirements pull you away from the intended customer.

**Boundary:** The concentric target diagrams distinguish perfect-fit buyers from increasingly mixed preferences. Their multipliers illustrate potential spillover, not a demand forecast. Complaints from your ideal customers should challenge your definition or product; complaints from mismatched buyers need different interpretation.
