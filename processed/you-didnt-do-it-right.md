# “You didn’t do it right”

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/you-didnt-do-it-right/) · Unofficial editorial note.

After four unsuccessful OKR implementations at WP Engine, Cohen challenges the assumption that every failure proves faulty execution. If advocates cannot describe when a framework is unsuitable, their explanation cannot be tested. He recommends a limited trial, then adaptation or replacement based on how the team actually works.

**Apply it:** Try a process with one team for a quarter and review its fit before expanding it. Cohen's alternative began with one metric the team cared about, then added a few after experience. His KPI diagram distinguishes work a product team controls from outcomes it only influences.

**Boundary:** This is an argument against universal prescriptions, not evidence that OKRs never work. Cohen explicitly allows that his own approach may need adjustment or fail elsewhere. The essay's illustrative fit percentage is not a measured success rate.
