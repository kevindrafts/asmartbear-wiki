# E01: Restarting growth when stuck at $32K MRR

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Dmytro Krasun.

Dmytro Krasun wants ScreenshotOne to grow beyond its current trajectory. Cohen separates the churn constraint, the size of plausible markets, and the founder's own appetite for continuing the business. [Discussion](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1/transcript).

- **Separate temporary use from recurring demand.** Cohen proposes comparing customers in their first hundred days with longer-lived customers. A completed short project and a failed ongoing workflow can both produce cancellation, but imply different acquisition budgets and product decisions. The suggested cutoff needs testing against the actual cohorts. [29:29](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1/transcript#t=29m29s).
- **Check whether a niche can deliver the desired result.** They work through potential hosting customers, attainable share, and monthly spending. Cohen doubts the optimistic share assumption: a useful campaign might improve revenue without becoming the company's whole growth strategy. The market counts are illustrative guesses. [44:35–53:13](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1/transcript#t=44m35s).
- **Give discovery protected time.** They consider improving the current business, finding another product using its technical strengths, selling, and testing AI-related demand. Krasun already has integrations but has barely promoted them. Cohen proposes a focused period of outreach and customer conversations, while keeping essential operations running; the deadline can move to accommodate meaningful evidence. [Existing integrations](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1/transcript#t=1h52m4s); [Time limit](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1/transcript#t=2h0m32s).

**Apply it:** Separate short-lived and recurring uses, test a candidate segment's economics, and reserve time to answer one consequential growth question. Spending to learn whether an opportunity exists is different from proving a scalable acquisition channel. [Discovery spending](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/1/transcript#t=1h55m31s). This checklist is an editorial application.

**Boundary:** The AI opportunity and alternative markets remain hypotheses, not a reported turnaround. Essential support and maintenance continue during exploration.
