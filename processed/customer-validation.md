# Yes, but who said they’d actually BUY the damn thing?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customer-validation/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen challenges founders to find prospective customers willing to discuss a concrete purchase before committing heavily to development. Personal need, friends’ encouragement, and a large theoretical market do not establish reachable demand. Difficulty finding interviewees is itself a commercial problem.

**Apply it:** Test a specific problem and price with real potential buyers.

**Boundary:** This 2010 essay emphasizes stated willingness. Read it alongside the later interview method, which makes clear that an agreeable answer is not proof of eventual purchase.
