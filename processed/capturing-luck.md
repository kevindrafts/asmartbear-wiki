# Capturing luck with “or” instead of “and”

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/capturing-luck/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen contrasts a business requiring many conditions to succeed together with one offering several alternative routes to an outcome. Multiple plausible channels can improve the chance of finding distribution, while marketplace models often add interdependent requirements on both sides.

**Apply it:** List indispensable assumptions separately from alternative solutions. Reduce avoidable dependencies and preserve affordable options.

**Boundary:** Having many options is different from executing all of them simultaneously. The probability example illustrates the burden of conjunctions; actual dependencies may be correlated, and optionality does not eliminate uncertainty.
