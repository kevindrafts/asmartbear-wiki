# Pricing determines your business model

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pricing-determines-your-business-model/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen treats price as an early business-design decision: it changes whom you sell to, what support you can afford, and how purchasing happens. His progression from free products to large enterprise contracts shows why the same feature set cannot simply acquire a different price tag without operational consequences. The middle can be awkward when enterprise selling costs arrive before enterprise-sized revenue.

**Apply it:** During discovery, ask about budgets, approval authority, alternatives, and the service a buyer expects. Choose a viable acquisition and support model alongside the product.

**Boundary:** The price bands are historical illustrations, not current market benchmarks or universal thresholds. Usage-based products can reach large account values without starting with a large-enterprise buyer.
