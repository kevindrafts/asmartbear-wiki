# Real Unfair Advantages

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/unfair-advantages/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Copyable features and ordinary hard work are weak defenses against a well-resourced rival. Cohen describes harder-to-reproduce assets: integrated industry knowledge, sustained excellence on a difficult dimension, earned authority, complementary teams, relevant endorsements, and learning from existing customers. These advantages accumulate through action; a new founder need not possess them all at launch.

**Apply it:** Identify what a competitor could reproduce quickly and what would require time, relationships, or uncommon capability. Invest deliberately in the latter while continuing to serve changing customer needs.

**Boundary:** Historical examples are not permanent competitive rankings. Authority and prominent advisers do not guarantee demand, an installed base can impede change, and an advantage still needs a viable product and business model.
