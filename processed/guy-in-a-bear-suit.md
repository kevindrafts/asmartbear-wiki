# Guy in a bear suit

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/guy-in-a-bear-suit/) · Unofficial editorial note.

At a software tradeshow, Smart Bear's costumed developer drew attention and then surprised visitors with technical expertise. Cohen describes how the mascot attracted conversation and made the booth memorable, including recognition at an awards ceremony. The useful combination was a distinctive presentation backed by someone who could discuss the product.

**Apply it:** Give an event's attention-getting element a natural bridge into a relevant conversation.

**Boundary:** This is an anecdote, with no measured conversion result. Its account of aisle congestion is part of the story, not a recommendation to obstruct attendees or ignore event rules.
