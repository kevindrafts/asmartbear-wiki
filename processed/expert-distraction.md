# Enough with the “expert” guilt

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/expert-distraction/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen challenges treating expert status or a fixed number of practice hours as prerequisites for useful work. Founders can develop competence while building a business, and innovation may enter areas where prior expertise is unavailable.

**Apply it:** Evaluate actual contribution and learning instead of waiting to qualify for an identity label.

**Boundary:** The essay does not dismiss skill or practice. It criticizes arbitrary thresholds and comparisons with exceptional performers, while acknowledging that effort, aptitude, and circumstance interact.
