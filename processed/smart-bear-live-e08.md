# E08: Creating customer interview questions before you have customers

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/8/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/8) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Rison Simon.

Cohen helps Rison Simon prepare interviews for PainPointy, a tool using Reddit discussions to inform marketing. The useful material is the revision process: separating what the founder believes from what a question can actually establish.

- **Separate the assumptions.** A candidate customer's role, company type, desired outcome, and purchasing motivation are different beliefs. Cohen and Simon disentangle these instead of treating one dense customer description as a proven segment. Keep the original beliefs visible so later learning is recognizable. [Method and segmentation](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/8/transcript#t=8m36s); [possible segments](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/8/transcript#t=16m23s).
- **Ask how work happens before suggesting your answer.** Cohen refines questions about the inputs used when revising marketing and how the person judges whether ideas will work. This can reveal uncertainty or missing evidence without prompting the interviewee to endorse Reddit research. [46:49](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/8/transcript#t=46m49s).
- **Separate initial usefulness from recurring demand.** A report can inform a campaign once without being needed again next month. The conversation treats repeat use as an unresolved part of the product hypothesis, not something a subscription price establishes. [Opening problem](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/8/transcript).

**Apply it:** Write one consequential assumption beside each neutral interview question, including what would change your mind. Ask about the participant's role and definition of success before inferring what your product should improve. This checklist is an editorial application of their preparation.

**Boundary:** This episode prepares interviews; it does not contain the resulting customer evidence or validate the proposed business. Numerical targets and candidate segments are working hypotheses. Official transcript wording and timestamps have not been checked against audio.
