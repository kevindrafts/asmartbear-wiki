# My family thinks I'm crazy for starting a business

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/my-family-thinks-i-m-crazy-for-starting-a-business/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer separates personal motivation from the need to win approval for starting a company. Cohen encourages founders to expect emotional difficulty, seek constructive feedback, and build relationships with people who understand the work. Others' discomfort does not settle whether the path fits the founder.

**Apply it:** Explain your motivations to yourself clearly, and seek a support network that can offer both encouragement and useful criticism.

**Boundary:** Conviction does not validate demand or remove financial and relational responsibilities. The response is encouragement about a personal choice, not evidence that every skeptical concern should be dismissed.
