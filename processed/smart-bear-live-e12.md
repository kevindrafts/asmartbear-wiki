# E12 w/ Tibo Maker: How can a $40K MRR SaaS be losing 20% of Its Customers

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Tibo Louis-Lucas, Jack Friks.

Post Bridge founder Jack Friks describes growth bursts followed by heavy customer losses. Cohen and Tibo Louis-Lucas examine what customers do before leaving, whom the product should serve, and how acquisition and pricing fit the founder's strengths. Friks says the newest marketplace cohort is too recent to judge its retention. [Conversation](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript); [New-cohort uncertainty](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=4m46s).

- **Separate loss patterns before choosing an intervention.** A sample of roughly 1,000 cancellations divides approximately equally into customers who never posted, stopped gradually, or were still posting when they left. Cohen proposes different investigations: help new users complete setup, intervene when activity declines, and interview active users before an apparently successful relationship ends. These categories describe canceled customers, not thirds of all subscribers, and do not establish their motives. [Cancellation sample](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=20m32s); [Active users](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=22m0s); [Setup milestones](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=25m31s).
- **Ground the target customer in retained users.** Cohen distinguishes losses outside the intended market from failures among customers the product should serve well. Tibo proposes comparing behavior with customer characteristics and acquisition channels. Friks clarifies a possible target: people already getting results from social posts who want to extend that work to more platforms. Cohen recommends interviewing retained users to make the description concrete. [Target clarification](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=40m22s); [Retention priorities](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=53m58s); [Segmentation](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=55m5s); [Customer evidence](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=1h17m27s).
- **Keep the disagreement about distribution.** Tibo hypothesizes that founder-focused promotion attracts people still experimenting, and emphasizes channels and content the founder can execute well. Cohen gives more weight to selecting a durable customer need and reducing losses. They agree the business must suit Friks, but do not establish which acquisition approach will work. [Channel hypothesis](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=28m27s); [Founder-channel fit](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=36m53s); [Closing distinction](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=1h19m7s).
- **Make pricing express the intended buyer.** Cohen questions whether beginner-oriented labels fit customers already succeeding with social media. Tibo proposes testing one per-account price with a minimum instead of closely spaced tiers; he acknowledges that the minimum can confuse small-account buyers outside the proposed target. The suggested amounts remain untested for Post Bridge. [Tier audiences](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=1h2m40s); [Simpler offer](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=1h7m34s); [Minimum qualification](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/12/transcript#t=1h9m5s).

**Apply it:** Compare cancellation behavior, customer fit, and acquisition channel before deciding whether to change setup, features, or marketing. Interview both retained customers and active users at risk of leaving. This is an editorial application.

**Boundary:** The reported 20% monthly loss concerns customers; it is not a verified revenue-churn rate. Growth arithmetic assumes other business inputs hold steady. Competitor figures and marketplace-ranking explanations are participant reports or hypotheses, not verified benchmarks or platform rules. No later outcome is reported.
