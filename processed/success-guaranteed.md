# The only way to guarantee startup success

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/success-guaranteed/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A story about Depeche Mode's landmark concert introduces Cohen's argument that a peak achievement cannot carry the meaning of years spent pursuing it. He locates a more durable definition of success in the work, relationships, and opportunities created during company building. Financial outcomes and metrics still matter, but cannot supply the entire purpose.

**Apply it:** Ask whether the daily experience and treatment of colleagues would remain worth endorsing if the hoped-for exit never arrived. Change what makes that answer negative.

**Boundary:** The title promises success only under the author's chosen definition, not business survival or guaranteed happiness. His generalizations about post-exit sadness are not a diagnosis or an inevitable emotional trajectory.
