# “I scratched my own itch” isn’t good enough

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scratched-my-own-itch/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Solving your own problem establishes useful experience and motivation, but Cohen rejects the leap from personal solution to viable business. Buyers may describe the problem differently, face different integrations, prefer an existing workaround, or lack urgency and budget. Building the code also leaves distribution, selling, and operating the company unproven.

**Apply it:** Separate what personal experience actually demonstrates from assumptions about other buyers. Interview those buyers about their circumstances and alternatives before extending the product around your own preferences.

**Boundary:** Founder experience is a starting advantage, not a disqualification. The article encourages learning capabilities that do not yet exist rather than requiring a first-time founder to arrive fully qualified.
