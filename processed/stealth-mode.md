# “Stealth mode” and other f’ing brilliant strategies

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/stealth-mode/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This satirical list offers deliberately bad startup advice: hide from customers, perfect the code before learning, assume calculated savings guarantee sales, copy competitors, and target everyone. Each reversal exposes an untested assumption that can feel reassuring to a founder avoiding commercial uncertainty.

**Apply it:** Turn the satire into questions about what you have actually learned from buyers, how you will reach them, and why they would choose this product.

**Boundary:** The headings are not literal recommendations. Its exaggerated claims about work intensity, funding, and competition should be read as rhetoric; the piece does not establish that every confidential project, funded launch, or balanced work schedule is mistaken.
