# Startup identity & the sadness of a successful exit

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/identity-selling-sadness/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Selling a company can remove a central source of identity even when the financial outcome is excellent. Cohen describes his own sadness after Smart Bear and relates other founders' accounts. Yet keeping the company does not preserve the original role: growth changes the work toward systems, managers, and responsibility for a larger organization.

**Apply it:** Consider both post-sale identity and the actual job of leading the next stage, rather than comparing an exit with an unchanged version of today's life.

**Boundary:** The personal account and cited studies do not establish that every founder reacts alike. Medical analogies are not diagnoses. Later editor's notes distinguish the original 2013 perspective from subsequent leadership changes.
