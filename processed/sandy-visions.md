# Sandy Visions

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/sandy-visions/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Watching tiny avalanches on a beach, Cohen reflects on the unlikely sequence behind each new customer. The excitement of a first sale can evolve into dependence on ever more abstract growth measurements. His closing image of crabs fighting over one scrap questions whether optimizing today's contest crowds out a larger opportunity.

**Apply it:** Alongside operating metrics, reserve attention for customer and employee experience and for opportunities the existing dashboard cannot express.

**Boundary:** This is a reflective essay, not a measurable allocation formula. It explicitly accepts the necessity of financial measures; the concern is allowing them to exhaust the company's imagination.
