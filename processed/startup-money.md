# Startup Exercise: What can’t be solved with money?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-money/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen separates problems money can accelerate from uncertainties it cannot resolve automatically. Funding can pay an identified team or expand an effective acquisition process, but it does not itself reveal what customers want or create authority. A credible investment case demonstrates learning already accomplished and explains which specific bottleneck capital removes.

**Apply it:** For each proposed expense, identify the proven mechanism it expands, the assumption still untested, and evidence that changed your prior decisions.

**Boundary:** The distinction is a heuristic: money can fund discovery work, but cannot guarantee its insight or result. Bootstrapped founders also benefit from this analysis because scarce funds make unexamined spending especially costly.
