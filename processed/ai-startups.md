# AI startups require new strategies: This time it’s actually different

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ai-startups/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that familiar startup advantages weaken when incumbents eagerly adopt the same AI technology. Existing distribution, data, budgets, and attractive research work can favor established firms. A chatbot using AI still competes for chatbot buyers; the size of a technology category is not its addressable market.

**Apply it:** Explain the customer problem, access to useful data, distribution path, and differentiation that survives competitors renting the same models.

**Boundary:** This is Cohen’s time-sensitive strategic assessment, not a prediction that startups cannot win. He allows that AI can increase spending and attract new buyers within an existing market.
