# In its emptiness, there is the function of a startup

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/emptiness/) · Unofficial editorial note.

As products, people, and scale change, Cohen asks what preserves a company’s identity. He proposes values that decide difficult trade-offs and let employees act without approval chains. Culture is the imperfect daily expression of those commitments.

**Apply it:** Write values as choices that affect real decisions, including choices that cost money or constrain talented people.

**Boundary:** A statement on a wall is insufficient without behavior. Financial sustainability remains necessary; Cohen’s argument is that it supplies the means for a worthwhile organization rather than its entire purpose.
