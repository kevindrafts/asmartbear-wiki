# Why startup biz dev deals almost never get done

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-biz-dev/) · Unofficial editorial note.

An attractive revenue share for a startup may be immaterial to a larger partner while putting that partner's customer trust at risk. Cohen illustrates this with small one-time plugin sales proposed to WP Engine. He recommends creating value through the larger company's established business model, such as bringing it recurring hosting customers, rather than simply requesting access to its audience.

**Apply it:** Calculate the partner's continuing benefit, implementation burden, and reputational exposure before proposing a joint offer. Identify the business owner whose existing objective improves.

**Boundary:** The article's percentage threshold and conversion assumptions are illustrative criteria, not universal deal rules. A strategic capability or customer benefit may matter beyond immediate revenue, but it needs a credible explanation.
