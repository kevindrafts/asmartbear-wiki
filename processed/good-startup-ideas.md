# The “Convergent” theory of finding truth in darkness

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/good-startup-ideas/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Polite enthusiasm can conceal incompatible ideas about who should buy a product, what it should do, and what it should cost. Cohen contrasts divergent feedback on an abandoned concept with converging pain, requirements, and payment commitments for WP Engine. His physics analogy distinguishes a fragile resemblance from an explanation that becomes stronger under further probing.

**Apply it:** Compare interviews for convergence on customer, problem, solution, and price; record actual commitments separately from compliments.

**Boundary:** Convergence is a judgment signal, not a physical law or numeric pass mark. Interview the relevant segment and resist selecting only evidence that protects a favorite idea.
