# Ignoring the Wisdom of Crowds

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/wisdom-of-crowds/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Aggregating independent estimates can work when errors offset and the question has an objective answer. Cohen contrasts that with creative selection, where accommodating every objection can remove the distinctive qualities that would delight a smaller audience. His voting and meal diagrams illustrate the difference between finding an answer and negotiating preferences.

**Apply it:** Use independent input to expose knowledge and alternatives, then decide whether the task is factual estimation or a coherent creative choice for a particular customer.

**Boundary:** Crowd accuracy depends on information, incentives, and correlated errors; it is not automatic. The simplified vote totals are expected outcomes, not guaranteed tallies, and gathering diverse feedback need not mean designing by unanimous veto.
