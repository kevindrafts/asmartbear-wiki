# What makes a strategy great

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/great-strategy/) · Unofficial editorial note.

For Cohen, strategy explains how a particular organization will win. A useful account simplifies complexity after confronting it, names uncomfortable realities, makes consequential choices, applies existing strengths, seeks favorable upside relative to downside, and addresses a distant outcome. Zappos illustrates how service, employee discretion, and returns policy reinforce one another rather than acting as isolated features.

**Apply it:** State the chosen customers, the advantages you will build, and the unwanted consequences you knowingly accept. Check whether a successful competitor could rationally choose the opposite. Separate supporting detail from the few decisions people must remember.

**Boundary:** Simplicity becomes harmful when it skips the difficult analysis. A roadmap, financial ambition, or hiring plan is not by itself a strategy. The vision-statement table illustrates destinations; it does not supply the decisions required to reach them.
