# Emotional rollercoaster

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/emotional-rollercoaster/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer brings together emotional strain, personal motivation, adaptation, decision discipline, and identity beyond a company. Success does not necessarily remove the psychological burden; growth can also require a change in the founder’s role.

**Apply it:** Revisit whether the venture is worthwhile and fixable, and use explicit decision reasoning during pressure rather than reacting only to the latest setback.

**Boundary:** These are reflections on entrepreneurship, not clinical guidance. Maintaining an identity beyond the business remains relevant before and after an exit.
