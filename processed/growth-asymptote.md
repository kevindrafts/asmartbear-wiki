# What to do when growth tapers off

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/growth-asymptote/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen diagnoses a growth plateau in sequence: customer losses, pricing, expansion within existing accounts, acquisition limits, and market saturation. The charts show why a fixed stream of new revenue eventually meets losses proportional to the installed base, and how expanding cohorts can counter that pressure. More acquisition cannot reliably repair a product customers abandon.

**Apply it:** Find the earliest broken mechanism before choosing a growth initiative. Investigate unmet expectations before cancellation, and tie upgrade charges to additional value.

**Source-date caveat:** The live page was readable on September 30, 2026, but its publication and modification metadata both say January 1, 2030. Those values are preserved as supplied, not treated as verified publication dates. It was found through article links outside the sitemap.

**Boundary:** His churn thresholds are contextual heuristics. Price rises can fail; channel changes may take years. A final question is personal: the owner may prefer profit, a changed role, or an exit over continued revenue growth.
