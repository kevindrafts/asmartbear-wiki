# Why I don’t like the LTV metric (LifeTime Value)

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ltv/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Lifetime value combines revenue, gross margin, and retention assumptions across a long future. Cohen argues that their instability makes the apparent precision misleading, especially when one worsening component is hidden by another improving one. He prefers examining each component and the gross-margin-adjusted acquisition payback period separately.

**Apply it:** Track customer acquisition cost, revenue per customer, service margin, and cancellations independently. Calculate payback using contribution after service costs, then test how the assumptions change.

**Boundary:** The criticism targets treating an estimate as stable knowledge. Payback thresholds in the essay are heuristics with explicit exceptions for mature businesses and larger customers; shorter-horizon metrics also require sound definitions.
