# The unfortunate math behind consulting companies

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/consulting-company-accounting/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen shows how salary-based hourly cost understates a consultancy’s cost per billable hour once utilization, leave, gaps, and development time enter the calculation. More employees also bring selling and management work. Internal products often stall because client work always takes precedence.

**Apply it:** Model realized billable capacity and overhead. Give a product explicit budget, ownership, and protected time as if it were a client.

**Boundary:** The dollar amounts and employment assumptions are historical illustrations, not current tax or compensation guidance. Contractors shift costs into their rates; they do not make those costs disappear.
