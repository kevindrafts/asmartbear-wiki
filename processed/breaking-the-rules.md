# Breaking the Rules

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/breaking-the-rules/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen contrasts Picasso’s classical skill with his later choices and examines Volkswagen’s deliberately negative headline. Both examples serve the same argument: departing from a convention is more credible when you understand what the convention accomplishes. The advertising example turns an apparent defect into evidence of inspection standards.

**Apply it:** State why a rule exists, then explain how your exception will achieve the underlying goal.

**Boundary:** Breaking convention is not evidence of insight by itself. The examples illustrate purposeful execution, not a claim that contrarian work will succeed.
