# Creating space

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/focus/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Focus means deliberately stopping activities so that the most consequential work gets enough attention. Cohen applies this to customers, metrics, differentiation, relationships, and management: resources spent serving poor fits or polishing everything cannot deepen the few strengths that matter. The article's repeated contrasts make exclusion the mechanism, rather than treating focus as greater effort.

**Apply it:** For a chosen priority, name the commitments to stop and the capacity that will move to it.

**Boundary:** The numerical improvements in the examples are rhetorical illustrations, not measured forecasts. Choosing a priority still requires judgment about impact and ability to execute.
