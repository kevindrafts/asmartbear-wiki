# What if there isn’t another 10x?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/another-10x/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen asks whether a mature company must keep pursuing another order of magnitude of growth. A valuable product can remain valuable after its strongest expansion opportunities diminish. Attention might then shift toward employee development, working conditions, community benefit, or other uses of accumulated capabilities.

**Apply it:** Separate an attractive existing business from the assumption that it must become dramatically larger. Define what additional impact would justify the next investment.

**Boundary:** The article includes a later retrospective on predictions first made in 2016. Those company examples are dated observations, and Cohen acknowledges that the next expansion opportunity cannot be ruled out with certainty.
