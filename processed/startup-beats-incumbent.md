# How startups beat incumbents

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-beats-incumbent/) · Unofficial editorial note.

An incumbent's assets also constrain it: existing customers resist disruptive changes, a large cost base raises the minimum worthwhile opportunity, and broad distribution requires readiness at scale. Cohen argues that startups can exploit a narrow segment, direct founder service, unusual technology, and a distinctive personality where the larger company cannot economically follow. A simpler product can win without matching every capability.

**Apply it:** Explain why your chosen advantage would be difficult or unattractive for the incumbent to copy, and identify customers who value it enough to accept your limitations.

**Boundary:** New technology and narrow scope carry risks of their own. The essay discusses noncompliance descriptively and explicitly disclaims advocating illegal conduct; its argument should not become permission to neglect security, law, or customer commitments.
