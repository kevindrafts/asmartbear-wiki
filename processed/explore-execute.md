# Explore vs Execute

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/explore-execute/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Product–market fit changes what the organization must become good at. Cohen contrasts searching for a repeatable business with delivering one reliably: experiments and improvisation give way to staffing, standards, coordination, and predictable service. His comparison table makes the same behavior look sensible in one stage and damaging in another. Rare incidents become routine at larger volumes, and hiring must precede overload.

**Apply it:** Identify which activities still need discovery and which now need dependable execution; match people and processes to each.

**Boundary:** This is a stage distinction, not a ban on later innovation. Cohen specifically warns against using a team's sprint velocity as a management productivity score.
