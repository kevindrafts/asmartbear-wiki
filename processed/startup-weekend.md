# Startup Weekend pep talk: It ain’t the code

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-weekend/) · Unofficial editorial note.

In this event talk, Cohen asks builders to spend their limited weekend on the business uncertainties they are least comfortable addressing. A working app is not enough; a clear pitch, conversations with prospective buyers, basic operating economics, and evidence of active demand reveal whether the project could become a company. The exercise also develops skills beyond coding and design.

**Apply it:** Use a short project to test a specific commercial assumption before adding implementation polish. Record what prospective customers actually did or said and how that changed the concept.

**Boundary:** A few interviews or email signups are early signals, not validated product–market fit or proof of payment. The competition's time limits explain the deliberately lightweight tests.
