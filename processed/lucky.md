# How much of success is luck?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/lucky/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that intentional work and favorable luck jointly explain success. Market timing, mentors, background, and opportunity helped Smart Bear, while measurement and decisions determined which opportunities were retained. Failed advertising was useful only because the company could identify and stop it.

**Apply it:** Distinguish the conditions you benefited from from the actions you can repeat. Build feedback into experiments so that good fortune can be recognized and poor results abandoned.

**Boundary:** Hard work does not guarantee success, and acknowledging luck does not erase skill. The autobiographical examples illustrate that interaction rather than quantify either contribution.
