# Too small to fail: How startups can grow in recessions

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startups-in-recessions/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Using historical examples of small software businesses growing during a recession, Cohen argues that a modest revenue goal changes the competitive problem. A tightly defined niche can offer accessible communities, room for expertise, and personal customer relationships without requiring a market large enough to interest a major incumbent.

**Apply it:** Size the business around the outcome you actually want, then test whether a specific audience has a persistent need and a reachable buying process.

**Boundary:** The title's resilience claim is deliberately emphatic. Two successful cases do not prove small firms are immune to recessions, competitors, or customer budget cuts; the many conditions in the argument still need validation.
