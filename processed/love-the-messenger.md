# Love the messenger

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/love-the-messenger/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A restaurant recommendation persuaded Cohen despite being far less specific than copy he would normally endorse. He attributes the result to a human interaction that conveyed knowledge and sincere enthusiasm. The story distinguishes an impersonal website's need to explain itself from the trust available in a live conversation.

**Apply it:** Equip customer-facing people to make informed, caring recommendations in their own voice, while keeping written product claims concrete.

**Boundary:** This is a single anecdote, including Cohen's assumptions about the waiter. Enthusiasm does not replace product knowledge or evidence. The source also includes a Korean translation of the same account.
