# Is it OK to Sucks?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/is-it-ok-to-sucks/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen recounts an advertising debate over edgy wording and later noticing similar language accepted elsewhere. He argues that a small company can gain memorability through personality, even at the cost of some audience approval. The advertisement ultimately used different wording and still performed well, complicating any claim that provocation was necessary.

**Apply it:** Test whether distinctive language helps the intended customer understand and remember the benefit.

**Boundary:** The story supplies no controlled comparison proving edgy copy wins. Deliberate tone should fit the audience and offer; offense alone is not differentiation, and once-unusual wording can become stale.
