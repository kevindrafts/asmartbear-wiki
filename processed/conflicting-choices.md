# “It’s a Balance” isn’t always the answer

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/conflicting-choices/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen offers different treatments for different conflicts: moderate two harmful extremes, choose between incompatible viable strategies, maximize one objective subject to a constraint, or invent a system that makes both possible. The activity-system example shows how several reinforcing choices can resolve an apparent contradiction.

**Apply it:** Classify the conflict before defaulting to compromise. If proposing both, name the additional capabilities and accepted disadvantages required.

**Boundary:** A middle market may be a deliberate choice, not automatically an unsatisfactory compromise. Inventing a combined solution carries real risk; historical business examples and personal-workload opinions are not universal prescriptions.
