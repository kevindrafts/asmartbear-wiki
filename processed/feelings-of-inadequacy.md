# Intense Asymmetry and Self-Flagellation

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/feelings-of-inadequacy/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Comparing two founders' results can smuggle in the assumption that their opportunities were equivalent. Cohen uses a counterfactual comparison of hosting businesses to show how timing, infrastructure, distribution, location, financing, and experience change the task. Selecting only the metrics on which someone else wins compounds the distortion.

**Apply it:** Before treating another company's growth as a verdict on your ability, list the differences in starting conditions and the advantages your comparison leaves out.

**Boundary:** Borrowing an idea from another business can be useful; using its outcome as a controlled comparison of personal worth is not. The essay's discussion of impostor feelings is personal commentary, not a clinical assessment.
