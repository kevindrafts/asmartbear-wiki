# How to motivate an employee

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/how-to-motivate-an-employee/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer asks whether apparent lack of motivation reflects a mismatch between the role and the person's goals. Cohen suggests examining growth opportunities, meaningful work, visibility of contribution, and connection to the organization's purpose before assuming unused potential will activate itself.

**Apply it:** Discuss what the employee wants from work and compare it with what the actual role offers. Identify concrete changes rather than relying on praise for hypothetical potential.

**Boundary:** If fit remains poor, separation may be appropriate for both parties. The short answer does not supply a performance-management process or employment-law guidance.
