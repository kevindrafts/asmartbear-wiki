# Specificity: A weapon of mass effectiveness

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/specificity/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen works through successive revisions of a bland marketing statement to show how specific language improves comprehension. Naming the behavior, audience, and kind of expertise gives readers something to picture and assess. Concrete examples also support application and humor, although they require more space than an abstract assertion.

**Apply it:** Find vague nouns and adjectives in a pitch, ask what observable thing each refers to, and substitute accurate details. Use an example where it clarifies the intended audience or consequence.

**Boundary:** Vivid wording is not evidence. The Twitter scenarios are historical workshop material and include deliberately invented comic examples; do not turn them into current channel recommendations or unsupported factual claims.
