# You’re a little company, now act like one

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/little-company/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Pretending to be a large established vendor can repel the early customers a small company actually needs. Cohen argues that early adopters may value direct founder access, rapid response, and influence over the product in exchange for tolerating rough edges. Generic corporate copy hides those real strengths.

**Apply it:** Describe the product honestly and make it easy for suitable early customers to start a conversation. Position for the buyers you can serve today.

**Boundary:** This advice concerns an early product seeking engaged adopters. It does not remove the requirements of enterprise procurement or justify promising features and reliability the company cannot deliver.
