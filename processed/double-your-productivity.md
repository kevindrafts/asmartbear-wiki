# Double your productivity without more work or stress

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/double-your-productivity/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen rejects extrapolating a small daily productivity improvement indefinitely. His travel-time puzzle shows how a slow part of a journey can dominate the total despite dramatic acceleration elsewhere. Time-use data then exposes routine activities consuming much of his week.

**Apply it:** Measure where time goes, reduce major sources of low-value work, and ensure the remaining major tasks are worthwhile.

**Boundary:** The suggested improvement is possible, not promised or endlessly repeatable. Faster execution of the wrong work still fails to address priorities.
