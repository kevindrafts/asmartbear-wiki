# The Code is your Enemy

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/code-is-your-enemy/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Technical founders may keep doing familiar product work while the business’s greatest uncertainties concern attention, buying, and demand. Cohen compares this with resolving the riskiest modules first in an engineering project. Customer conversations also expose whether the founder can reach the intended audience at all.

**Apply it:** Identify the unresolved commercial assumption and assign it deliberate time before another comfortable code improvement.

**Boundary:** Functional software remains necessary. This is a corrective for founders overinvesting in building, not an argument to disregard product quality or stop engineering once customers arrive.
