# Reframing “Freemium” by charging the marketing department

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/freemium/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen treats a free tier as an acquisition investment whose infrastructure and support costs belong in the marketing calculation. Free users can provide reach, social proof, and future upgrades, but their preferences may overwhelm the feedback of likely buyers. Multiplying visitor-to-free and free-to-paid conversion exposes the actual paid-customer yield.

**Apply it:** Calculate the full cost per acquired paying customer, including service to nonconverters, and compare it with alternative channels. Segment feedback by customer fit and willingness to pay.

**Boundary:** Conversion benchmarks in the essay are historical examples, not universal forecasts. Viral distribution or other strategic benefits can justify freemium, but should be valued explicitly rather than assumed free.
