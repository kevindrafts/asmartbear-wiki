# E07 w/ Dan Martell: 1 big logo won't get him the next 10

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Dan Martell, George Paladichuk.

George Paladichuk reports that one franchise brand supplies about 90% of NaiL's revenue. Cohen and Dan Martell work on finding additional buyers for its AI receptionist, while questioning whether the existing deployment demonstrates a repeatable, differentiated product. [Customer concentration](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript#t=1m50s).

- **Define the buyer more precisely than enterprise.** Paladichuk settles on home-service franchise brands with at least twenty operating locations. The hosts also emphasize willingness to adopt new technology; size and industry alone do not establish that. Martell favors exploring adjacent buyers where existing experience and references remain useful. [Adjacent buyers](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript#t=15m47s); [Franchise definition](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript#t=23m17s).
- **Look for observable clues to harder-to-see traits.** Martell describes using other technology choices as signals of early adoption. Cohen highlights the distinction between the desired characteristic and a correlate that is easier to find. These examples suggest research methods, not universal filters: a social account or software choice does not prove buying readiness. [24:42–28:55](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript#t=24m42s).
- **Learn how this organization authorizes a purchase.** Interest from a brand president does not establish permission from its holding company. Cohen asks who decides, on what evidence, and through which process; Martell discusses consultative qualification and limited paid pilots. Expanding to sibling brands may grow revenue while leaving the same parent-level concentration risk. [Buying mechanics](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript#t=38m27s); [Concentration qualification](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript#t=50m56s).
- **Prove what makes the niche real.** Martell challenges whether NaiL has a repeatable product or a service tailored to one customer. A franchise label is insufficient without capabilities those buyers particularly value. They propose investigating adjacent workflows and integrations rather than claiming the differentiation is already established. [57:29–1:03:22](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/7/transcript#t=57m29s).

**Apply it:** Name the target buyer, verify the approval path, and test whether additional independent customers want substantially the same product. Track shared ownership when assessing diversification. This is an editorial application.

**Boundary:** Business figures are participant reports; benchmark sales cycles, market counts, and concentration thresholds are speaker heuristics. The discussion proposes a sales direction but does not report new wins or verify the holding company's plans. Transcript wording has not been checked against audio.
