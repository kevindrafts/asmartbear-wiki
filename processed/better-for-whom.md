# Better for whom?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/better-for-whom/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A product cannot be meaningfully better without specifying whose needs define the comparison. Cohen uses contrasting project-management use cases to turn a generic superiority claim into distinct possible businesses: beginners, complex organizations, mobile work, open communities, or changing requirements.

**Apply it:** Complete the claim with a particular customer and situation. Use that definition to choose interview subjects, features, messages, and acquisition channels.

**Boundary:** These examples are hypothetical business proposals, not validated opportunities. A precise target makes the idea testable; it does not prove demand.
