# Zero-sum marketing channels: Good or bad for a startup to pursue?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/zero-sum-marketing/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen distinguishes scarce advertising or ranking positions from channels where several companies can build useful audiences. Contested slots can become expensive and impose a ceiling even on the winner. Smaller firms may find more room through distinctive content, relationships, or long-tail demand than by bidding directly against well-funded incumbents.

**Apply it:** Examine whether a channel's growth depends on displacing a rival from scarce inventory, and compare that constraint with approaches that expand your own audience over time.

**Boundary:** Non-zero-sum is a relative characterization, not unlimited attention or independence from platforms. Social media still has algorithms and competition, while paid channels can work when their economics and available volume fit the business.
