# How do I know where to advertise?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/begin-advertising/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen’s experience with many advertising campaigns left him skeptical that intuition could reliably identify the best channel. A print placement that initially worked later deteriorated, illustrating why even past success needs continued measurement.

**Apply it:** Prefer an experiment whose business outcome is measurable, whose feedback arrives quickly, and whose cost is affordable. Familiarity with the audience can help shape the creative. Seek a limited trial before scaling.

**Boundary:** This advice concerns finding an acquisition model under uncertainty. It does not promise that the cheapest channel will scale or that a previously productive placement remains productive.
