# The rise of the “successful” unsustainable company

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/unsustainable-companies/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen challenges a definition of startup success centered on fundraising and investor exits when the underlying businesses do not endure. He also distinguishes that pattern from companies with real customers and growth whose economics or markets prove less durable than expected. The desired alternative combines growth with retention, useful products, and values enacted in operations.

**Apply it:** Explain how customer value and operating economics would sustain the business after financing excitement subsides. Distinguish shareholder liquidity from lasting organizational health.

**Boundary:** The named-company judgments and prices are historical commentary, and Cohen acknowledges hindsight-based speculation. They are not current investment assessments; neither bootstrapping nor outside funding alone determines sustainability.
