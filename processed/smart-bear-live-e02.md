# E02 w/ Rob Walling: $1M ARR, how to expand into a new market

[Read the official transcript](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/2/transcript) · [Episode and audio](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/2) · Unofficial editorial note.

Participants: Jason Cohen, Rob Walling, Jesse Schoberg.

DropInBlog has an established self-service business and early sales to larger customers. Cohen and Walling explore how to expand using evidence from its strongest customers, rather than treating company size alone as a strategy.

- **Find the buyer before rewriting the message.** An engineer attending a demo may be evaluating a tool for a marketer who discovered it. Cohen separates those roles; Walling suggests inspecting search intent as another clue. Messages and tests should address the person at each stage, not assume the demo attendee explains the whole journey. [36:35–43:29](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/2/transcript#t=36m35s).
- **Compare customer quality, not just volume.** Schoberg reports better retention and less support friction among his BigCommerce cohort than among his larger Shopify cohort. The hosts favor investigating that existing strength. These are observations about DropInBlog's customers, not verdicts on either platform's entire market. [55:55–1:01:08](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/2/transcript#t=55m55s).
- **Make expansion somebody's job.** Cohen proposes an owner, budget, and separate measures for each chosen growth effort, because existing customers otherwise consume the available attention. For agency relationships, Walling also emphasizes dependable support and protecting the agency's client relationship; commissions are only one possible benefit. [1:48:19–1:56:00](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/2/transcript#t=1h48m19s); [2:04:10–2:05:28](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/2/transcript#t=2h4m10s).

**Apply it:** Map discovery, evaluation, and purchasing roles for a promising segment. Choose one expansion test, name its owner, and budget enough protected time to learn. This is an editorial application of the discussion.

**Boundary:** The call proposes a direction, not a completed growth result. Walling explicitly warns against copying this response to a plateau with a different cause. Account-based marketing and suggested price points are conditional on economics and capability. Official transcript wording and timestamps have not been checked against the audio.
