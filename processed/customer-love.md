# How to get customers who love you even when you screw up

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customer-love/) · Unofficial editorial note.

An early Smart Bear customer tolerated defects because he trusted Cohen’s responsiveness and helped shape a tool that made him effective at work. His internal advocacy grew from both useful results and personal involvement. Pretending to be a larger company would have weakened that relationship.

**Apply it:** Be candid about your capacity, keep promises, and understand what success looks like for the person championing you.

**Boundary:** This is an early-adopter relationship, not evidence that all customers welcome unfinished software or that one customer’s requests should determine the entire roadmap.
