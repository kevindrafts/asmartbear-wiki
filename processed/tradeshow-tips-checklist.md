# A Tradeshow Checklist, born of experience

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/tradeshow-tips-checklist/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen treats a trade show as both a costly acquisition experiment and an unusually concentrated source of conversations. His checklist spans defining a primary goal, preparing clear messaging and reliable demos, arranging meetings, staffing the booth, recording context, and following up. Repeated live demonstrations also reveal confusing language and product gaps.

**Apply it:** Choose a measurable purpose, prepare for practical failures such as unreliable internet, and capture enough conversation context to make follow-up useful. Feed what you learn into the product and pitch.

**Boundary:** Historical costs, channel tactics, and anecdotes are not current event benchmarks. A crowd or a bag of contact details is not qualified demand; adapt participation and outreach to accessibility, consent, and venue rules.
