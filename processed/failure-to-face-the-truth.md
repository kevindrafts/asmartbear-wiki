# Failure to face the truth

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/failure-to-face-the-truth/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen connects weak strategy and dysfunctional teams to the avoidance of uncomfortable facts. Trust makes candid disagreement possible; specific positioning forces exclusions; a useful strategy acknowledges the actual obstacle. His retelling of Kroger and A&P contrasts acting on unwelcome market evidence with suppressing it. The charts illustrate their subsequent divergence, rather than proving a universal causal rule.

**Apply it:** State the difficult fact a plan currently sidesteps, then identify what decision would change if the team accepted it.

**Boundary:** Candor needs care and trust. This essay synthesizes other authors' frameworks and historical accounts; those examples are reported by Cohen, not independently verified here.
