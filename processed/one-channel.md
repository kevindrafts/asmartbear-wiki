# Marketing at early startups: Deep, not wide

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/one-channel/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that an early company's limited time is better spent making one suitable channel work deeply than maintaining weak activity everywhere. Familiarity with a medium's culture and language improves the chance of communicating naturally and effectively. Additional channels follow once the first is working and approaching its available reach.

**Apply it:** Select a relevant medium you understand, commit enough effort to learn its economics, and identify its realistic ceiling.

**Boundary:** The essay's universal phrasing is stronger than its anecdotal basis. This is an early-stage focus heuristic; it does not remove later saturation or platform-concentration risks.
