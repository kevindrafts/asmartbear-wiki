# Strong opinions, somewhat weakly held

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/strong-opinions-weakly-held/) · Unofficial editorial note.

An outsider's suggestion can expose an unquestioned habit or disturb a message refined through years of evidence. Cohen resolves that tension by varying the burden of proof with the basis of the existing decision. Accidental choices should move easily; well-tested choices deserve proportionate scrutiny before replacement.

**Apply it:** Record why a decision exists and what evidence supports it. Evaluate a proposed change against those reasons while remaining open to a genuinely better explanation.

**Boundary:** Time invested is not itself evidence of correctness. Brand consistency should not become a shield against contrary results, and skepticism should test new ideas rather than exclude newcomers.
