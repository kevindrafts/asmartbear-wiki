# The wrongness of relativism

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/relativism/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Exceptional exits can turn successful founders into dissatisfied spectators, and comparisons with average companies can produce the same mistake. Cohen argues for evaluating a metric through the trade-offs available to this business: improvement may consume innovation, employee well-being, customer value, or the founder's preferred life. The relevant decision is contextual rather than a contest against a headline valuation.

**Apply it:** For a troubling benchmark, state what changing it would cost and whose goals it would serve before choosing a target.

**Boundary:** External benchmarks can still expose a problem worth investigating; they cannot choose priorities for you. The Instagram, WhatsApp, and Slack examples reflect the article's explicitly historical 2014 setting, not current valuations.
