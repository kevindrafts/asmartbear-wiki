# Communicating Values: Show, don’t Tell

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/show-dont-tell/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Adjective-heavy marketing promises too many virtues without making any credible. Cohen argues that values become believable through operational evidence: a return policy, an answered phone, or specific customer testimony. An organization can then explain the action instead of asking the audience to trust an unsupported self-description.

**Apply it:** Replace one generic brand claim with observable proof and a concrete customer consequence. Check that sales, support, and hiring behavior support the same promise.

**Boundary:** The examples illustrate consistency rather than universal policy requirements; every company need not offer the same guarantee. Words remain useful when they explain evidence, and curated testimonials should be presented honestly.
