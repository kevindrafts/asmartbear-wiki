# Selecting the right product metrics (KPIs)

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-metrics/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen's later metrics framework maps activities and outcomes by timing, control, and distance from the customer. Immediate team work, usage, operational health, and downstream financial or customer success can all matter without being interchangeable. The diagram also separates metrics to improve from standards to maintain.

**Apply it:** Give each metric a plain-language purpose, precise definition, owner, update cadence, and a clear consequence when it changes. Check that a convenient proxy measures the intended behavior.

**Boundary:** This explicitly revises his earlier preference for one central metric. Lagging outcomes remain important, but they are unfair or uninformative when treated as the only evidence of near-term work.
