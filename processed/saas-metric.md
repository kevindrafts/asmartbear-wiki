# No wait, of course THAT is the single most important SaaS metric

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/saas-metric/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen deliberately makes a persuasive case for several incompatible candidates for the most important SaaS metric, including retention, growth, profitability, acquisition efficiency, and satisfaction. The reversal is the lesson: a metric serves a company's present objective and stage rather than ruling every company at every moment. Focus remains useful, but universal metric supremacy does not.

**Apply it:** Identify the next milestone and the constraint threatening it, then choose a small set of measures with explicit trade-offs.

**Boundary:** The opening sections are rhetorical arguments, not blanket endorsements of every causal claim or historical valuation statement. Read them alongside Cohen's criticism of long-horizon LTV estimates and his later, multidimensional treatment of product metrics.
