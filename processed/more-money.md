# More money if you do, more money if you don’t

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/more-money/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cash needs can exceed plan when growth is slower or faster than expected. Slower revenue leaves committed costs uncovered; faster acquisition requires onboarding, staffing, and acquisition spending before recurring receipts catch up. Cohen argues that fundraising around a single precisely forecast cash trough ignores both paths.

**Apply it:** Model slower and faster growth scenarios, including the timing of service and acquisition expenses, before deciding how much runway is sufficient.

**Boundary:** The essay assumes a business that spends ahead of recurring revenue. Its call for additional capital is not a universal instruction to raise venture funding or continue an unviable plan.
