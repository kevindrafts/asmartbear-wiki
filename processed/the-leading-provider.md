# The leading provider of meaningless marketing solutions

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/the-leading-provider/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen dissects corporate descriptions that use many words while concealing what the company actually does. Generic leadership claims and vague promises of solutions neither identify the product category nor explain a meaningful difference. He sees this language as a symptom of avoiding the harder work of understanding customers and choosing a clear position.

**Apply it:** Ask an unfamiliar reader to explain your product after one sentence. Replace empty status claims with a recognizable category, a specific customer, and a useful distinction.

**Boundary:** Category language helps only if the intended audience understands it. The historical company examples illustrate communication failures rather than judgments about those products' underlying quality.
