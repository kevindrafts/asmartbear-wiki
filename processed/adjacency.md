# Adjacency Matrix: How to expand after PMF

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/adjacency/) · Unofficial editorial note.

After fit, expansion should reuse existing strengths without breaking the working business. Cohen’s matrix asks how much marketing, sales, service, product, engineering, and the business model must change. His enterprise example shows similar technology concealing very different commercial and service requirements.

**Apply it:** Classify each function’s change as minor, manageable, or a substantial rebuild before selecting an expansion. Use the matrix to expose risks and explain the decision.

**Boundary:** It evaluates feasibility rather than choosing the largest opportunity. Several major organizational changes require a deliberately large, risky investment; they are not ordinary incremental development.
