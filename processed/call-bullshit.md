# Do I dare call bullshit aloud? Do you?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/call-bullshit/) · Unofficial editorial note.

An unsolicited offer to publish affiliate-driven guest posts prompts Cohen to examine why he avoids confrontation. He identifies fear of losing access to influential people as a pressure toward insincere politeness and public agreement.

**Apply it:** Decide what editorial standards and professional disagreements you will stand behind before a request tests them.

**Boundary:** This is a personal, provocative account, not a general outreach playbook. Its value lies in recognizing approval-seeking; the angry response and threats in the anecdote are not necessary to establish a firm boundary.
