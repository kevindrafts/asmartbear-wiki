# How to simplify complex decisions by cleaving the facts

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/complex-decisions/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen separates potential gains from liabilities so a decision does not circle endlessly among incomparable arguments. Choose for the strength needed to achieve the outcome, then reject or redesign options whose weaknesses cannot be managed. The product diagrams contrast developing an exceptional advantage with merely making every weakness average.

**Apply it:** Identify the few decisive dimensions, evaluate upside, and specify downside mitigation.

**Boundary:** A weakness can veto an option. Simplification is a decision aid, not permission to hide constraints, and irreversible choices deserve more investigation.
