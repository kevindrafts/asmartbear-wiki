# Solving the Low-Budget Online Marketing Dilemma

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/low-budget-marketing/) · Unofficial editorial note.

For cash-constrained marketing, Cohen asks whether a working channel has enough unused inventory to justify further optimization. If its ceiling is near, a new channel may matter more. Website improvements can help all incoming traffic, while very small samples make tiny measured gains unreliable. He recommends testing changes large enough to matter and leaving clearly poor channels early.

**Apply it:** Estimate available channel volume, identify shared conversion bottlenecks, and define a meaningful effect before spending the next small budget.

**Boundary:** The early-exit rule is explicitly based on experience rather than data. Larger funded teams can afford parallel exploration; this essay addresses constrained budgets, not every growth organization.
