# Letters to Joel Spolsky

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/letters-to-joel-spolsky/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A fictional sequence of letters moves from rejecting a prominent writer's supposed invitation to apology, flattery, escalating demands, and an absurd imagined intrusion. The unstable narrator exposes insecurity and hunger for recognition while also expressing admiration for Spolsky's influence. Cohen credits Steve Almond's earlier literary structure.

**Apply it:** Read this as satire about status and self-justification, including how a person's story about another's silence changes to protect their ego.

**Boundary:** The letters are a comic construction, not evidence that the depicted invitation, conduct, or technical intrusion occurred. They are not an outreach template.
