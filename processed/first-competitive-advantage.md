# When being “first” is not a competitive advantage

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/first-competitive-advantage/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Being first records a historical event; customers still need a reason to choose the product now. Cohen contrasts that claim with experienced benefits such as reliability and support. Pioneers also carry legacy architecture, promises, and pricing that later entrants can avoid. First entry helps when the company converts its lead into lasting advantages while continuing to improve.

**Apply it:** Replace a claim of primacy in positioning with a customer-visible advantage and explain how the lead strengthens over time.

**Boundary:** This began as a 2011 competitive argument about WP Engine and includes later editorial updates. Its company comparisons are historical, interested examples rather than current vendor evaluations.
