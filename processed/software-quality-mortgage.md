# Software Quality Mortgage

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/software-quality-mortgage/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen likens early shortcuts to borrowing against future software quality. Shipping quickly can help a young company learn and survive, while recurring defects later consume capacity like interest without repairing the underlying principal. Once many users depend on the product, deeper maintenance becomes part of sustaining delivery rather than an indulgence for engineers.

**Apply it:** Connect proposed refactoring to recurring incidents and slower change, allocate time to address their causes, and track whether customer-visible problems decline.

**Boundary:** The mortgage is an analogy, and the prevention multiplier is a proverb rather than measured evidence. It does not justify avoidable security or reliability harm, nor imply every rewrite is a sound repayment plan.
