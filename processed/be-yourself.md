# Being who you are, while becoming better

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/be-yourself/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen treats identity as compatible with deliberate improvement. His account of procrastination shows how a trait can be acknowledged without declaring it desirable. The same internal drive that creates a company can also produce overcommitment, distraction, exhaustion, and perfectionism.

**Apply it:** Decide which shortcomings need accommodation and which obstruct the work you care about. Protect focused commitments and genuine time away.

**Boundary:** Constant improvement is not required in every activity. The article distinguishes enjoyment from competitive achievement and presents striving as part of Cohen’s own temperament rather than a universal lifestyle prescription.
