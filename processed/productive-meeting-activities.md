# Productive meeting activities: Leverage the team, empower the individual

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/productive-meeting-activities/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A useful meeting depends on the kind of help being requested. Cohen distinguishes emotional processing, generating possibilities, supporting a decision, and stress-testing work. Confusion arises when participants supply critique to someone seeking understanding, or new options to someone trying to choose. Collective input can improve a decision without transferring ownership to an undefined group.

**Apply it:** Name the conversation's purpose and decision owner in advance, share needed material, and redirect contributions that belong in another mode.

**Boundary:** The framework supports collaboration rather than unilateral disregard of colleagues. A manager can participate without taking over the responsibility intentionally assigned to someone else.
