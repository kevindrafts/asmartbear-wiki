# Building in public forces true competitive advantage

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/building-in-public/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen presents public work as a constraint that can improve craftsmanship and force a business to find advantages beyond hidden code. Marketplace participation illustrates an asset that access to the software alone would not reproduce. Public compensation rules similarly push a company to compete for talent through its work environment.

**Apply it:** Ask what would remain valuable if competitors could see the implementation, and invest in that asset.

**Boundary:** Technical advantages can sometimes endure, and secrecy can sometimes be appropriate. The article offers an incentive argument; public visibility does not itself guarantee quality, a strong culture, or a durable moat.
