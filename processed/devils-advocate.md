# Rude Q&A: The constructive devil’s advocate

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/devils-advocate/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen adapts Scott Berkun’s adversarial questioning exercise to expose weak assumptions and sharpen a pitch. Difficult questions about differentiation, pricing, durability, and customers force clearer answers than a friendly rehearsal.

**Apply it:** Ask a trusted challenger to surface the questions you least want to answer, then practice a calm, substantive response.

**Boundary:** The exercise may include unfair premises to prepare for them; that does not make those premises true. Unanswered questions are material for further work, not automatic reasons to abandon the idea. The author offers an official skill.
