# Rare things become common at scale

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scale-rare/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A failure that is unusual for one server becomes routine across a large fleet. Cohen follows the consequences beyond infrastructure: notifications fail, new customers form poor first impressions, support investigates, and public complaints require context. Aggregate incident volume can rise even while an individual customer's service improves.

**Apply it:** Forecast the operational and communication work created by more customers, including exceptional cases and specialized staffing, rather than extrapolating today's direct costs alone.

**Boundary:** Automation is necessary but does not erase unfamiliar incidents or human coordination. The server example illustrates expected frequency, not a guarantee of evenly spaced failures; scale does not excuse declining customer experience.
