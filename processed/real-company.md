# You’re a real company when…

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/real-company/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This personal essay asks when a growing business starts to feel tangible. Cohen first considers administrative and commercial milestones, then recalls the pride of seeing a company sign installed. A later addition connects WP Engine's building to a parking-garage ramp remembered from childhood. The meaning comes from the founder's history rather than a standardized scale threshold.

**Apply it:** Notice concrete moments that make shared work feel real, and let a team recognize progress beyond financial dashboards.

**Boundary:** Office signage and printed books are memoir details, not required investments or measures of business quality. The page explicitly juxtaposes an older account with a 2023 reflection; its customer and circulation figures belong to those historical updates.
