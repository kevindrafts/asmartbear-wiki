# Product Purgatory: When they love it but still don’t buy

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/purgatory/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen separates two reasons enthusiastic prospects never purchase. Some dislike the operational risk even if installation and price disappear; others want the outcome but cannot prioritize it now. His email-security example illustrates how solving a genuine problem can introduce a more frightening dependency. Escaping the second trap requires finding customers with a present trigger, such as a strategic initiative or pressing constraint, rather than polishing a generally appealing pitch.

**Apply it:** Ask what competing project would lose resources if this purchase happened, then test whether that urgent segment is identifiable and reachable.

**Boundary:** Urgency cannot rescue an unaffordable or inaccessible segment. The article's accessibility examples describe buying behavior, not a reason to neglect accessibility, and its market-size multiplier is illustrative rather than a forecast.
