# “ROI” is the wrong way to sell your product

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/roi-selling/) · Unofficial editorial note.

At Smart Bear, time-saving calculations failed to close deals even when customers requested them. Cohen explains the disconnect through budget ownership: hypothetical savings in developers' time did not release money from a tools budget. Buyers responded to a convincingly better workflow, while purchasing constraints still governed what they could spend.

**Apply it:** Identify the budget owner, the permitted category of spending, and the outcome that person recognizes. Use a demonstration to make improvement tangible, then test willingness to pay within the actual purchasing process.

**Boundary:** The historical code-review example does not prove financial justification is universally useless. Real cash reductions and procurement requirements differ from assigning an hourly value to time that remains on payroll.
