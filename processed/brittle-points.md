# Brittle Points: How to make companies robust

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/brittle-points/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A healthy company can still fail when a single platform, customer, employee, or acquisition channel disappears. Cohen’s server analogy shows why redundancy costs money and can introduce new dependencies. Diversification can sometimes improve growth and resilience together.

**Apply it:** Maintain a dependency inventory and tackle the most useful risk reduction next, considering cost and whether it funds later improvements.

**Boundary:** Young companies cannot remove every dependency immediately. Robustness changes with scale, and the personal equivalent is not running several companies at once: Cohen advocates a life with meaningful successive chapters rather than identity wholly dependent on one business.
