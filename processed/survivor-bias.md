# Business Advice Plagued by Survivor Bias

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/survivor-bias/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Advice drawn only from winners can mistake traits shared with failures for causes of success. Cohen illustrates missing observations, unpublished null results, and the apparent exceptional individual found after many attempts. He explicitly applies the warning to his own repeated startup successes rather than claiming exemption from it.

**Apply it:** Ask which failed or unobserved cases would test a recommendation, whether the outcome was selected after the fact, and what mechanism makes the advice relevant to your circumstances.

**Boundary:** Awareness reduces an interpretive trap but does not eliminate selection bias. The aircraft illustration is identified as hypothetical, and the essay's historical stories are examples, not independent validation of every business conclusion.
