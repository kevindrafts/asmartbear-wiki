# The Startup Drake Equation

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-drake-equation/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen compares startup survival with a chain of prerequisites: demand, distribution, acceptable economics, delivery, retention, funding, and a team able to continue. A strong product alone cannot compensate for a fatal missing condition. He recommends choosing a market suited to the founders, designing around weaknesses, cultivating exceptional strengths, and investigating the remaining existential uncertainties first.

**Apply it:** List the conditions your business requires, separate demonstrated capabilities from assumptions, and run the next experiment against the most consequential unresolved one.

**Boundary:** This is a risk inventory inspired by the Drake equation, not a calibrated probability model. Factors interact; references to strengths exceeding certainty are metaphors, and even excellent specialists do not make execution risk disappear.
