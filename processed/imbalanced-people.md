# Imbalanced People

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/imbalanced-people/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Exceptional contributors can create ideas and experiences that adding more average capacity would never produce. Cohen extends this argument beyond engineering to design, writing, management, support, and sales. His sales example emphasizes enthusiastic customers and company-wide confidence, not simply more calls.

**Apply it:** Describe the qualitative improvement a hire should create, alongside the volume of work they will handle. Consider whether the company's story and environment can attract that person.

**Boundary:** The claim of infinite superiority is rhetorical, not a measured productivity ratio. The essay champions excellence but does not supply a selection method or justify disregarding collaboration and context.
