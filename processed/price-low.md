# Should I price my product low?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/price-low/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer allows low pricing when market reach, operating costs, acquisition efficiency, and owner goals support it. A small price can work with a large reachable audience or a sufficiently lean business, but it requires more customers for the same revenue. The position should communicate useful value rather than embarrassment about an unfinished product.

**Apply it:** Model the customer volume and cost structure needed at the proposed price before choosing it.

**Boundary:** The source question contains missing price amounts; this note does not reconstruct them. Its market-size examples are illustrative, and low price alone does not establish demand or affordability of support.
