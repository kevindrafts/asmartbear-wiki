# Max MRR: Your growth ceiling

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/max-mrr/) · Unofficial editorial note.

With fixed monthly new recurring revenue and a positive constant loss rate, the steady-state revenue ceiling equals new MRR divided by that rate. Cohen's charts show current revenue approaching this ceiling, while changes in acquisition or losses move the ceiling earlier than they visibly change total revenue. His Buffer case illustrates dynamic customer movement beneath a flat headline.

**Apply it:** Track the model's inputs and implied ceiling together, using consistent revenue-based definitions. Test whether retention or acquisition changes would materially raise it.

**Boundary:** This is a conditional equilibrium, not a guaranteed forecast. Cohort expansion, varying acquisition, and market limits change the model; the source's NRR equation is not used here as a production-ready definition.
