# Not disruptive, and proud of it

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/disruptive/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that serving a recognized need can create a valuable business without overturning an industry. Novel behavior can be hard to explain and sell, and the inventor of a breakthrough may not capture its returns. His own companies improved established activities.

**Apply it:** Evaluate utility, reachable demand, differentiation, and viable economics before seeking a disruptive label.

**Boundary:** He does not oppose ambitious innovation. The choice depends on resources and goals; a later note adds WP Engine as another example of substantial growth without making disruption the founding purpose.
