# Navigating the unpredictability of everything

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/predict-the-future/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A strategy provides direction without requiring an accurate script of the future. Cohen's product stories show customers using tools in unexpected ways, revealing a better business than the original plan. He proposes robustness through alternative routes, existing strengths, durable advantages, simpler dependencies, hedges, and investment in needs unlikely to disappear. Scenario diagrams widen attention beyond a single forecast.

**Apply it:** Record assumptions, observe actual customer behavior, and define options that keep one mistaken prediction from becoming fatal.

**Boundary:** An unlikely event occurring does not by itself invalidate a probability forecast, and one exceptional company does not refute base rates. The historical examples motivate adaptation rather than prove that all forecasting is useless.
