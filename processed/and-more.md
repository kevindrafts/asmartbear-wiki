# Bed, Bath, Linens, Things, Beyond, and More!

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/and-more/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that vague catch-all wording weakens a business’s clearest selling point. A specialist in rain barrels becomes less distinctive when it suggests an unspecified assortment instead of showing commitment to its specialty. Even a genuinely broad assortment needs a message that gives the reader a reason to care.

**Apply it:** Replace an ambiguous ending with the specific offering, audience, or enthusiasm that matters to your customer.

**Boundary:** The article allows that memorable phrasing can have value. Its target is evasive messaging, not a prohibition on diversified businesses.
