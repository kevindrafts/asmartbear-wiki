# If you build it, they won’t come, unless…

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-marketing-ideas/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A list of standard channels and A/B tests does not explain why anyone will notice a startup. Cohen explores stronger mechanisms: meaningful transparency, sharing required by product use, a story suited to its audience, a repeatable paid-acquisition model, and relevant relationships. His Smart Bear example improves when it explains why code review matters to the listener rather than merely describing software features.

**Apply it:** State the mechanism by which attention becomes qualified demand, then test that mechanism. Give optimization a promising message and audience to work on.

**Boundary:** These are candidate approaches, not a checklist every company needs. Celebrity reach does not guarantee product fit, intrinsic sharing is not the same as a share button, and acquisition economics must include real costs.
