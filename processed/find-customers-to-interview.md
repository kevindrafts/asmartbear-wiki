# When you have nothing: How to find potential customers to interview

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/find-customers-to-interview/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen treats finding interviewees as a deliberate acquisition problem before a product exists. Start with a plausible customer profile, reduce scheduling friction, offer something valuable, and ask each participant for referrals. Communities, complaints, professional networks, small tools, events, and paid recruitment provide different access routes. People who have already tried workarounds offer stronger problem evidence than casual complaints.

**Apply it:** Pick one or two recruitment methods, record response and referral rates, and turn brief useful conversations into deeper follow-ups.

**Boundary:** Clicks validate some messaging, not a whole business. Investors supply ideas and introductions rather than customer proof; paid participants still need screening. Respect community rules and contribute before asking.
