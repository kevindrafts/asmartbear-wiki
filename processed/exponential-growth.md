# The Elephant in the room: The myth of exponential hypergrowth

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/exponential-growth/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that impressive growth is often mislabeled exponential. His charts distinguish constant percentage growth from constant additions and increasing additions. Individual campaigns and markets accelerate, mature, and decline; overlapping them can produce an aggregate resembling a quadratic curve. Market saturation and revenue per customer can explain a company's changing trajectory better than extrapolating its headline rate.

**Apply it:** Forecast market size, share, and monetization separately. Track absolute additions alongside percentages, and begin new growth experiments before existing channels flatten.

**Boundary:** The fitted curves and company examples are descriptive historical models, not laws guaranteeing a quadratic future. Component timing, market capacity, pricing power, and declining campaigns matter; an attractive fit alone does not establish the mechanism.
