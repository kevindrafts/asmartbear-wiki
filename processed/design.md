# On the (un?)importance of design

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/design/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A dramatic WP Engine redesign improved pride and customer impressions without an obvious change in the displayed conversion or engagement charts. Cohen contrasts that experience with companies whose design is central to preference.

**Apply it:** Decide whether design is part of how your product wins, then invest consistently with that decision.

**Boundary:** The original account is from 2011. A 2025 note says expectations have shifted toward stronger design in business software. Flat measured results do not establish that design has no value, including unmeasured effects.
