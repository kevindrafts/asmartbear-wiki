# Reputation isn’t as powerful as you imagine

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/reputation/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen's announcement of WP Engine to a large blog audience produced surprisingly few purchases. He distinguishes an audience's interest in its author from its need for a particular product. The more consequential advantage was recruiting: public writing exposed his approach and helped attract an experienced WordPress collaborator. Reputation can therefore improve a company's inputs without functioning as a ready-made customer list.

**Apply it:** Identify which specific constraint your reputation could relieve—trust, hiring, introductions, or demand—and measure that channel instead of multiplying followers by an assumed conversion rate.

**Boundary:** This is a historical launch account, not evidence that audiences never sell. Audience–product overlap matters, and the article's later success annotation should not erase the uncertainty of its original 2010 setting.
