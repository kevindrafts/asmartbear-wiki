# If Kindergarten were like Social Media Marketing

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/social-media-marketing/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This satire moves social-media advice into a kindergarten classroom. Formulaic authenticity, attention-grabbing headlines, relentless publishing, networking compliments, and popularity counts produce awkward results when children take them literally. The humor exposes the distance between a tactic that sounds sensible and a relationship that actually works.

**Apply it:** Examine whether a communication habit creates value for its audience or merely imitates a popular formula.

**Boundary:** The children and incidents are fictional illustrations, not case studies. The essay supplies a critique of mechanical behavior, not a tested alternative marketing program.
