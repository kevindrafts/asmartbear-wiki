# Hello, I’m 1074018628

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/hello-support/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen contrasts claims of customer care with the experience of actually seeking help. Promotional mail, inaccessible contact routes, and representatives without authority can make service function as a barrier. Knowledgeable people who can resolve exceptions make the promise credible.

**Apply it:** Try the support path as a customer and check whether the first person reached has the knowledge and authority to help.

**Boundary:** This short essay offers a service principle through examples, not a staffing model or a requirement that every company use phone support.
