# Employed with a side of startup

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/working-startup/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A day job can finance early experimentation, but the side business must fit limited and irregular availability. Cohen recommends a narrow product, realistic service promises, gradual growth, and clear separation from the employer's time and resources. He also emphasizes resolving ownership and employment conflicts rather than relying on secrecy.

**Apply it:** Choose a model you can support honestly, protect focused working time, and review the relevant agreements and permissions before committing resources.

**Boundary:** The author explicitly says he is not providing legal advice. His sample letter and statements about employment agreements do not determine rights across jurisdictions; this note does not reproduce that template or substitute for tailored legal review.
