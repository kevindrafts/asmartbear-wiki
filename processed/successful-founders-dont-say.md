# 10 things I’ve never heard a successful startup founder say

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/successful-founders-dont-say/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This short ironic list challenges reassuring founder myths: that the first idea needs no customer correction, hiring removes operational difficulty, secrecy fools early buyers, or generic marketing drives results. Cohen ends by emphasizing contact with prospective customers and actual delivery over prolonged debate about startup doctrine.

**Apply it:** Notice which comfortable belief is delaying a concrete test with customers.

**Boundary:** These are rhetorical contrasts rather than survey findings. Legal structure and intellectual property can matter in specific circumstances; the essay's point is that administrative or philosophical preoccupation cannot substitute for creating a viable business.
