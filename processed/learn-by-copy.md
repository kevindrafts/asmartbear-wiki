# Learn by Copy

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/learn-by-copy/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen describes practicing other writers' structures and styles to discover his own preferences and limitations. Trying a terse inspirational style taught him that he wanted to leave readers with a usable method; other experiments revealed structures he enjoyed but could not readily invent. Imitation becomes a learning exercise whose result is a more intentional voice.

**Apply it:** Study how an admired piece works, practice its technique with your own material, and record what fits your goals.

**Boundary:** The essay explicitly distinguishes learning from style and structure from taking another person's words. Attribution and the rights of the original creator still matter.
