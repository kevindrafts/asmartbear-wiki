# Who should my target customer be?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/who-should-my-target-customer-be/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer recommends defining a particular buyer through a recognizable problem, awareness of that problem, willingness to address it, and a reachable channel. It connects an initial customer profile to interviews and a focused first marketing effort, with adjacent segments considered after the initial fit becomes clearer.

**Apply it:** Describe a plausible buyer's circumstances, test that description in conversations, and choose a channel where those people already seek help.

**Boundary:** The market-size figures and promised breadth beyond the ideal profile are heuristics linked to longer essays, not minimum requirements or guaranteed multipliers. A small sustainable business may need a different market than a venture-scale company.
