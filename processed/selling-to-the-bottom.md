# Selling to the Bottom

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/selling-to-the-bottom/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Smart Bear's code-review tool gained advocates by removing tedious coordination from developers' daily work. Cohen contrasts that bottom-up route with selling reporting benefits to executives who might impose the tool on reluctant users. Even in an executive meeting, he preferred demonstrating the improvement to developers' lives over presenting speculative financial savings.

**Apply it:** Identify who bears the work of adoption and give that person a reason to champion the purchase. Show decision-makers how user value supports their goals.

**Boundary:** A later annotation connects the story to product-led growth, but this was a historical enterprise-software sales experience. End-user enthusiasm alone does not resolve procurement, security, or budget constraints.
