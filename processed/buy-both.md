# When customers buy your competitor’s product… and then buy yours

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/buy-both/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A hospital using expensive monitoring software also bought a smaller tool because it made a frequent task easier and fit an easy purchasing route. Cohen learned both procurement limits and a useful advertising channel by asking about the customer’s other products. Small purchases later expanded.

**Apply it:** Investigate an individual user’s workflow, approval friction, and discovery habits before assuming a sale requires replacing the incumbent.

**Boundary:** The historical prices are not current procurement thresholds. Selling a small tool to an enterprise employee differs from winning an enterprise-wide contract; the same approach will not fit every buyer.
