# Maybe not so much with the “optimization”

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/creativity-over-optimization/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen’s miniature code-change viewer was harder to implement and less space-efficient than conventional alternatives, yet users cared strongly about it. He uses that example to defend product taste and delight that incremental optimization may miss.

**Apply it:** Leave room for a distinctive experience people may love, and listen for evidence that it drives preference.

**Boundary:** The essay still supports objective feedback and cutting corners where appropriate. One successful indulgence is not a reason to treat every expensive aesthetic choice as strategic.
