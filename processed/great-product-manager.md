# The “Great” Product Manager, a.k.a. the Impossible Product Manager

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/great-product-manager/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen rejects the expectation that one product manager can excel at every part of the job. His diagram places strategy and customer understanding before the decision about what to build, with backlog execution and cross-company coordination afterward. He groups these into four capabilities and argues that a team can collectively supply the excellence that no individual has time to provide.

**Apply it:** Map ownership and strengths across all four capabilities before hiring another generic PM. Design complementary roles, including contributions from research, engineering, program management, and product marketing.

**Boundary:** His one-excellent, one-good rule is an experience-based heuristic. Role boundaries depend on the organization; the goal is coverage, not title conformity.
