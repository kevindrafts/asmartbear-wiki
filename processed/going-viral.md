# Darwinian advice for “Going Viral”

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/going-viral/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A message must travel beyond the people who already know its creator. Cohen's reproduction analogy emphasizes recipients who can and want to pass it onward, not merely first-generation attention. Ready-to-share wording and a clear request reduce the work of helping.

**Apply it:** Give an advocate a concise explanation that remains meaningful to someone with no prior relationship to you. Make sharing easy without making it the entire content.

**Boundary:** Cohen acknowledges that his own viral essays spread because readers valued them, not because these mechanics guaranteed success. The old platform examples illustrate friction reduction; they do not establish a formula for popularity.
