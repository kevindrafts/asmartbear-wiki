# How do I stop “analyzing” and pick between two good choices?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/business-tiebreaker/) · Unofficial editorial note.

When both funding paths are genuinely viable and their trade-offs are understood, Cohen recommends choosing the work and company you want to experience. Continuing analysis has a cost because it blocks spending, hiring, and other decisions.

**Apply it:** Compare the daily responsibilities each option creates and ask which feels fulfilling.

**Boundary:** This tie-breaker assumes two rational options; preference does not repair broken economics. Later disappointment cannot prove that the unchosen path would have been better.
