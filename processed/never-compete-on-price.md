# Sometimes never compete on price

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/never-compete-on-price/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Low prices become dangerous when they are the only remaining difference between similar products. Cohen contrasts reactive discounting with systems deliberately designed for affordability. His retailer, airline, furniture, and investment-fund examples connect operational choices and accepted limitations to a lower cost structure. Those limitations can make the system harder for rivals to imitate.

**Apply it:** Explain which activities and tradeoffs make your low price economically sustainable, including acquisition and service costs.

**Boundary:** Historical examples and financing figures are the author's case studies, not current operating facts. A price cut needs more than an equal percentage increase in volume to preserve revenue, and an even greater response may be needed to preserve profit.
