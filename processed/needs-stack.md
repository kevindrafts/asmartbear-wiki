# Using the Needs Stack for competitive strategy

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/needs-stack/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A product usually supplies a means to a higher customer goal. Cohen builds a chain from infrastructure through a website and audience to a speaking career, showing how a solution higher in that chain can remove demand for lower-level tools. Higher-level products often trade flexibility for faster achievement of a narrower goal.

**Apply it:** Map the customer's immediate task and next-level objective. Explain concrete features at the level they are shopping, connect benefits one level above, and measure whether that outcome improves.

**Boundary:** Moving upward may require a different business, narrower market, or an outcome the vendor cannot control. Education or adjacent features may be more realistic than a complete pivot.
