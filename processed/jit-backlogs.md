# JIT selection from independent streams: An alternative to the “big backlog” of work

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/jit-backlogs/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen separates deciding what matters from deciding what can happen next. Sales, support, engineering, and distinct customer needs can each maintain an ordered list using relevant criteria. Work planning then selects among the strongest candidates while accounting for dependencies, capacity, risk, and deadlines. The diagram examples show multiple streams of evidence feeding one execution plan.

**Apply it:** Ask each group to rank its own needs, then explain cross-stream choices and track whether important categories are being starved.

**Boundary:** Separate lists do not remove the hard allocation decision or promise every stakeholder delivery. They organize the inputs; strategy and judgment still determine the actual schedule.
