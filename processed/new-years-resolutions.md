# New Year’s Questions (neé Resolutions)

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/new-years-resolutions/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen replaces generic annual resolutions with questions tailored to business stage. Early companies should seek payment and honest market signals; established small firms can examine pricing, retention, and worthwhile spending; scaling firms need alignment, hiring, and preparation for future constraints. Consultants are asked what would justify materially higher rates.

**Apply it:** Choose the question that exposes the largest unresolved constraint at your current stage and turn it into an observable next step.

**Boundary:** The prompts are a historical reflection exercise, not an annual planning system or a claim that the same growth target suits every owner.
