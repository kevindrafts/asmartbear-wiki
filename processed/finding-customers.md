# How do I find customers to talk to?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/finding-customers/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer starts interview recruitment with a concrete customer profile. Introductions, targeted professional outreach, relevant gatherings, online communities, and landing pages are routes to those people. Cohen recommends requesting expertise and investigating actual experiences with open questions, then revising the hypothesis as evidence accumulates.

**Apply it:** Describe whom you need to meet well enough that a contact can name someone; ask interviewees for further introductions.

**Boundary:** Recruitment success is not product validation. The unusually strong WP Engine outreach response is an anecdote from a particular context, not an expected conversion rate for every founder.
