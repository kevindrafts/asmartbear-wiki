# Tech Support is sales

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/tech-support-is-sales/) · Unofficial editorial note.

For many software customers, support is the company's only human voice. Cohen argues that helping people accomplish their work can create trust, purchases, retention, and advocacy more effectively than treating support as a low-status cost center. Repeated conversations also reveal mistaken terminology, broken expectations, and potential changes in the product's direction.

**Apply it:** Resolve the immediate issue, then investigate why it occurred and what outcome the customer was pursuing. Give support enough knowledge and authority to help, and route recurring lessons into product decisions.

**Boundary:** The Smart Bear experience supports a particular service-led sales model, not the claim that every business can eliminate sales. Support conversations also miss people who leave silently or never try the product.
