# The Lindy Effect on startup potential

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/lindy-effect/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen uses a doubling heuristic to distinguish plausible continuation from a much larger growth ambition. A channel that won today's customers may reach a similar number again, yet lack enough inventory for a tenfold business. Substantial expansion therefore needs evidence of strong momentum, a new mechanism, or an adjacent market.

**Apply it:** For a large growth target, identify what must change and how that change will supply the additional demand and delivery capacity.

**Boundary:** The article's extension of the Lindy effect is an analogy, not a statistical guarantee that a business will double or that a project is halfway finished. Later historical charts illustrate one forecast rather than validate a universal rule.
