# Discover what’s blocking sales with less than a day of work

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/more-sales-customer-feedback/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Existing customers survived the buying process, so their feedback can miss the barriers that stopped everyone else. Cohen proposes optional contact collection, trial and uninstall prompts, compensated conversations, reviews, and in-product feedback routes to learn from nonbuyers. Testimonials can also reveal a benefit the company's own pitch overlooks.

**Apply it:** Add one low-friction feedback route and ask about the actual evaluation journey, including where interest ended. Distinguish repeated obstacles from isolated requests.

**Boundary:** The tools and campaign examples are historical. Participation should be voluntary; incentives should purchase time and candor, not favorable reviews. The essay supplies tactics, not proof that every additional form improves conversion.
