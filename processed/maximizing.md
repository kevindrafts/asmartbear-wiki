# Satisficing vs Maximizing

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/maximizing/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Searching for the best possible option consumes time that a merely adequate choice could save. Cohen distinguishes decisions worth extensive exploration from routine work where a clear acceptable threshold is enough. Strategy often deserves sustained scrutiny; small operational tasks usually benefit from speed.

**Apply it:** Declare whether a decision seeks excellence or sufficiency before comparing options. For sufficiency, define the threshold and stop investing once it is met.

**Boundary:** Neither approach is universally superior. Perfectionist habits can make low-value decisions expensive, while rushing a durable strategic choice can impose much larger costs later.
