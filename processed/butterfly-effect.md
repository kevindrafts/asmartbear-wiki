# A butterfly flaps its wings and you make a sale

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/butterfly-effect/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen questions whether subtle psychological effects deserve attention amid the many competing influences on a buyer. He favors a compelling message, meaningful customer interaction, and distribution over hoping a minor design change transforms outcomes.

**Apply it:** Address obvious barriers to attention and purchase before optimizing small signals.

**Boundary:** A later editorial note says many cited psychology results failed replication. Preserve that correction: the examples are not reliable evidence for marketing tactics. Nor does the essay establish that all small design details are worthless.
