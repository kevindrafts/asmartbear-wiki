# Disentangling the three languages: Customers, Product, Business

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/three-languages/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Customers describe their work and desired outcomes; product teams describe research, features, and delivery; executives describe financial performance and resource commitments. Cohen argues that apparent disagreement often comes from failing to translate among these perspectives. Product management connects customer context to executable work, then explains how that work contributes to business results shared with other functions.

**Apply it:** Trace a proposed feature from a customer's situation through the work required to an observable business contribution. Make dependencies and responsibility explicit.

**Boundary:** Revenue reflects more than product effort, but that does not exempt product teams from demonstrating impact. The source table is a translation aid, not a demand that every function adopt the same metrics or planning rhythm.
