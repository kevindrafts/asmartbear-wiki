# Pivot Points

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pivot-points/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A persistent trait or constraint can help in one setting and hinder in another. Cohen calls these facts Pivot Points and recommends identifying them before labeling them strengths or weaknesses. For companies, observed behavior, recurring customer feedback, architecture, and values provide evidence; independent idea gathering followed by clarification helps separate observation from immediate solutions.

**Apply it:** Design a role or strategy around the facts currently true, then identify which limited changes would justify a substantial investment.

**Boundary:** The points are relatively fixed for near-term planning, not permanent identities. Verify them, preserve specificity, and avoid changing many at once. The full source and official workshops provide the detailed exercises.
