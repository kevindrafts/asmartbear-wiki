# You can have two Big Things, but not three

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/two-big-things/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen argues that major commitments compete for limited time and attention even when described as work–life integration. His deliberately stark limit on concurrent priorities forces a choice about what will receive sustained effort and what will be neglected. He explicitly excludes sacrificing sleep as a viable way to create capacity.

**Apply it:** Map demanding commitments against realistic weekly capacity and discuss the consequences with affected people before adding another.

**Boundary:** The fixed number is a provocative heuristic, not an empirically established limit. People differ in support, resources, and obligations; the useful question is what your actual schedule and relationships can sustain.
