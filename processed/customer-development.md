# The Iterative-Hypothesis customer development method

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customer-development/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen’s interview method starts with learning goals and written hypotheses, then uses open questions to seek disconfirming evidence. Record answers, revise assumptions, and investigate surprises. His WordPress examples show how migration friction and differing security priorities changed the business concept.

**Apply it:** Ask about actual workflows, purchasing triggers, budgets, alternatives, and customers’ own language. Separate emerging segments and walk through proposed use in detail.

**Boundary:** Stop when meaningful learning is exhausted, not after a few agreeable conversations. Stated intent is weaker than actual use. Cohen allows explicit price testing but warns against selling throughout the interview; use the official workshop for the author’s full process.
