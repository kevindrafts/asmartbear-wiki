# How repositioning a product allows you to 8x its price

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/more-value-not-save-money/) · Unofficial editorial note.

The same efficiency improvement can be sold as a smaller bill or as greater output for an acceptable unit cost. Cohen's hypothetical advertising tool illustrates why a growth-focused buyer might value additional qualified leads more than budget savings. Positioning should match the result the buyer is already responsible for achieving.

**Apply it:** Identify the buyer's actual objective, then explain the product's contribution in that objective's terms rather than defaulting to time or cost savings.

**Boundary:** The argument assumes room to increase spending and useful output. The source's second chart alt text conflicts with its prose amounts; this note preserves the mechanism without repeating the inconsistent figures or universalizing its growth-value claims.
