# How to select your first marketing channel

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/first-marketing-channel/) · Unofficial editorial note.

A useful first channel lies where reachable customers overlap with the founder's abilities and genuine interest. Cohen argues that excelling in a smaller relevant community can outperform mediocre participation in the largest one. Once one channel works, revenue and reusable material can support expansion. If there is no overlap, a complementary partner—or a different business—may fit better.

**Apply it:** Map where target buyers already seek information, then choose one channel you can sustain and learn deeply.

**Boundary:** Personal preference alone is insufficient: customers must actually be there. Platform examples illustrate the selection method, not permanent claims about where every occupation congregates.
