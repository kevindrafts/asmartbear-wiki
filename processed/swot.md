# Replacing the “SWOT” with The Landscape

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/swot/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen criticizes SWOT exercises that produce generic lists without decisions. A fact such as a simple product or a large customer base can help one strategy and hinder another, so labeling it a strength or weakness too early conceals the real trade-off. He instead begins with salient facts, constraints, and their implications for a coherent way to win.

**Apply it:** For each planning observation, explain which strategic option it enables or blocks, what makes it distinctive, and what action follows. Separate routine operating problems from the competitive choice.
