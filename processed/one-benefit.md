# Pick one and own it

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/one-benefit/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen proposes defending a product through one strong advantage to sharpen its pitch. His imagined sales conversations show how premium service, quality, or direct access to a small team can answer several objections through one coherent promise. The exercise also reveals which operating behaviors must support the claim.

**Apply it:** Choose a customer-valued advantage and rehearse skeptical questions without retreating into a long feature list. Use specific evidence for every promise.

**Boundary:** These are hypothetical sales scripts, and Cohen repeatedly declines to guarantee they will work. Their portrayals of competitors and open source are arguments within the exercise, not facts to repeat about actual alternatives.
