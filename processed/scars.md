# Scars

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scars/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Experience can become both identity and a blind spot. Cohen describes applying a bootstrapper's cost-saving instinct after WP Engine had funding intended to accelerate growth. The same apparently attractive return on his time could be inferior to another opportunity under the new circumstances. He distinguishes enduring values from habits that merely feel consistent with the past.

**Apply it:** Name the principles you will keep even at a cost, then ask trusted advisers which operating habits no longer serve those principles or the current company.

**Boundary:** Changing behavior does not require abandoning identity. The revenue alternative in the essay is a hypothetical comparison, not a measured result proving that growth work always beats cost reduction.
