# The roadmap to Product/Market Fit (PMF)… maybe

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-market-fit-formula/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Cohen connects founder strengths, a plausible market, customer discovery, a small complete product, distribution, retention, prioritization, and founder psychology into a route toward fit. Discovery can invalidate a business assumption cheaply, but observed product use provides different evidence. Retention work should compare unsuccessful users with successful ones rather than merely collecting traits that both groups share.

**Apply it:** Write the current milestone and its main obstacle, then direct product and commercial work toward that obstacle. Diagnose whether a cancellation reflects a broken promise or a poorly selected customer.

**Boundary:** WP Engine followed this sequence; Smart Bear found its successful product through shipping and successive pivots. Cohen explicitly acknowledges that interviews might have prevented the latter path. The framework is a repeatable aid to judgment, not a necessary sequence or success guarantee; the historical growth charts illustrate those different paths.
