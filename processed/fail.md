# Stop saying “fail”

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/fail/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Calling every setback a failure hides what happened and what to do next. Cohen distinguishes exploration, skill development, optimization, ordinary problem solving, and major strategic mistakes. A rejected hypothesis can be useful evidence; a serious misjudgment may call for a redesign rather than another small adjustment.

**Apply it:** In a review, name the specific kind of setback and the next action it implies. Separate the result of an experiment from a judgment about the person running it.

**Boundary:** Language should clarify responsibility, not disguise damage. Cohen retains the word failure for genuinely final outcomes such as a company closing before meaningful success.
