# Excuse me, is there a problem?

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/problem/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Solving a genuine problem does not ensure a viable company. Cohen traces the additional conditions: enough reachable prospects, recognition and urgency, budget, ability to buy now, preference for this vendor, and continuing value. His multiplicative worksheet exposes how one weak condition can overwhelm a large apparent market. Narrowing the customer can improve several conditions even while reducing the audience.

**Apply it:** Test each condition for one specific buyer, product, and price; investigate the weakest link before building more.

**Boundary:** Cohen explicitly calls the score a rough thinking aid, with exceptions for niche and owner-income businesses. The normalization and historical examples are not validated success probabilities, and continued payment is not the sole form of repeat value.
