# Advice for a first-time manager

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/advice-first-time-manager/) · Unofficial editorial note.

This mailbag answer reframes a new manager’s contribution as building a team that performs well without constant intervention. Individual expertise becomes less useful if every decision still depends on the manager. Cohen emphasizes clear communication, hiring lead time, and coverage when someone leaves or is unavailable.

**Apply it:** Identify where the team relies on one person, recruit complementary expertise, and make room for others to own outcomes. Treat repeated corrections as a reason to examine both hiring and your own interference.

**Boundary:** His high hourly valuation of founder time is a prioritization prompt, not a measured wage. Redundancy and hiring ahead of demand consume cash; the answer describes the transition toward scalable execution rather than a staffing formula for every startup.
