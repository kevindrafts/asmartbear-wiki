# Scaling by “delegation” isn’t good enough

[Read Jason Cohen’s original](https://longform.asmartbear.com/delegation/) · Unofficial editorial note.

Replacing a founder’s labor with people instructed to work exactly the same way limits the organization to the founder’s abilities. Cohen instead wants teams with stronger expertise, ownership, and the ability to improve themselves. Shared capability also reduces dependence on individuals. The test of scale is whether the team can make sound decisions without the founder, rather than merely complete assigned work.

**Apply it:** Hire for capabilities beyond your own and make teams responsible for outcomes, not merely assigned tasks.

**Boundary:** Empowerment still entails accountability and leadership. The challenge becomes managing specialists whose work you cannot personally outperform, rather than withdrawing from management.
