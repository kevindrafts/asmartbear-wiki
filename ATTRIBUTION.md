# Attribution and editorial policy

Jason Cohen writes the primary source articles at
[A Smart Bear](https://longform.asmartbear.com/). This wiki is independent commentary,
not a replacement for those articles or a career-wide archive.

Each note links directly to its original, preserves source publication and
modification metadata separately, and binds its editorial review to a body hash.
Sitemap last-modified dates are discovery metadata, never publication dates.
Topic pages combine readings from the same author; this is not independent corroboration.
Decision sequences are editorial applications unless explicitly attributed otherwise.
Historical prices and company examples are contextual, not current benchmarks.

Full HTML, extracted text, and any inspected source images remain in an external
cache. They must never enter this repository, Git history, the built website, or
its search index. Test fixtures are synthetic. Prefer original explanation to
quotations; any direct quotation must be short, attributed, and checked against
the source. Do not copy diagrams; explain their relevant meaning and link readers
to the original. An unclear image requires inspection or an explicit unresolved flag.

The author's official [workshops](https://skills.asmartbear.com/) cover founder
fit, ideal customers, customer interviews, and pricing. Their
[repository license](https://github.com/asmartbear/asb-skills/blob/main/LICENSE)
was identified as CC BY 4.0 on 2026-09-30. This project links to those materials;
it does not reproduce them.
