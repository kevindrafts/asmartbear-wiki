# Build a sustainable small business

**Define enough.** Read [bootstrapping](../topics/bootstrapping.md) and [fulfillment](../topics/fulfillment.md), then choose the income and responsibilities the business should support. [Include your own labor](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ramen-profitable/); [Fit the work to the person](https://longform.asmartbear.com/fulfillment/).

**Make the economics visible.** Use [pricing](../guides/price-product.md), [cash flow](../topics/cash-flow.md), and [SaaS economics](../topics/saas-economics.md) to separate recurring revenue from profit and spendable cash. [Collection timing](https://longform.asmartbear.com/annual-prepay/); [Inspect the components](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ltv/).

**Protect capacity.** Read [sustainable work](../topics/sustainable-work.md) and [delegation](../topics/delegation.md). More activity is not necessarily more useful output. [The cost of overload](https://longform.asmartbear.com/utilization/).

**Checkpoint:** Does the business support its promises and its people without a hidden ongoing subsidy? Growth is one possible next choice, not the only definition of success.
