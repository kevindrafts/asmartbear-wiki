# Exploring a company idea

**Start with your constraints.** Read [Founder–market fit](../topics/founder-market-fit.md), then examine which assumptions your experience establishes and which remain untested. [Personal experience is a starting point](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scratched-my-own-itch/).

**Find a reachable buyer.** Work through [selecting an ICP](../guides/select-customer.md) and [customer interviews](../topics/customer-interviews.md). The destination is a revised buying hypothesis, not a collection of encouraging reactions. [Learning that changes the idea](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customer-development/).

**Make a small commitment.** Use [version one](../guides/first-version.md) to test a complete narrow outcome, and [pricing](../guides/price-product.md) to connect it to an operating model. [A small complete product](https://longform.asmartbear.com/slc/); [Price shapes the business](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pricing-determines-your-business-model/).

**Checkpoint:** Can you name the next uncertainty to test, the person affected, and the evidence that would change your mind? If not, more features may only deepen an unexamined commitment.
