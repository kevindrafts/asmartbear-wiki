# Scale a company that already works

**Reassess the constraint.** Begin with [stalled growth](../guides/stalled-growth.md), even if revenue is still increasing. The installed base changes the impact of losses and expansion. [Where growth tapers](https://longform.asmartbear.com/growth-asymptote/).

**Adapt the operating model.** Read [scaling](../topics/scaling.md), [hiring and management](../guides/hire-and-manage.md), and [technical debt](../topics/technical-debt.md). New coordination and continuity needs cannot be solved by preserving every early habit. [The changing organization](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scale/).

**Align independent teams.** Use [strategy](../topics/strategy.md), [choosing next work](../guides/choose-next-work.md), and [metrics](../topics/metrics.md) to connect decisions with outcomes. [Shared choices](https://longform.asmartbear.com/strategic-choices/); [Different kinds of evidence](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-metrics/).

**Checkpoint:** Are you adding reliable organizational capability, or merely more people and projects? Test expansion across every affected function before calling it an adjacency. [The cost of expansion](https://longform.asmartbear.com/adjacency/).
