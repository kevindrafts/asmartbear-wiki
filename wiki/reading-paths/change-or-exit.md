# Reconsider the role, direction, or ownership

**Separate identity from the next decision.** Read [founder psychology](../topics/founder-psychology.md) and [founder–market fit](../topics/founder-market-fit.md). A role that once fit may no longer do so. [Identity and changing work](https://longform.asmartbear.com/identity-selling-sadness/); [Reassessing persistent facts](https://longform.asmartbear.com/pivot-points/).

**Compare the available paths.** Use [persist, pivot, or stop](../guides/persist-or-pivot.md) to judge the next investment, then [the acquisition guide](../guides/consider-exit.md) if an actual offer exists. [Past effort and future value](https://longform.asmartbear.com/sunk-costs/); [A concrete sale decision](https://longform.asmartbear.com/rich-vs-king-sold-company/).

**Include other people's futures.** Read [company culture](../topics/company-culture.md) and [exits](../topics/exits.md). Employees and customers may experience the same change differently from the founder. [A wider view of consequences](https://longform.asmartbear.com/acquisition-failure/).

**Checkpoint:** Is your choice based on a realistic future and explicit values, or on preserving a past identity? The essays offer perspectives, not a prediction or a substitute for transaction-specific advice.
