# From first customers to repeatable demand

**Learn from both sides of the purchase.** Read [sales](../topics/sales.md) and contact people who tried but did not buy. Successful users alone cannot explain every conversion barrier. [The missing buyers](https://longform.asmartbear.com/put-down-the-compiler/).

**Choose a repeatable route.** Follow [first-channel selection](../guides/first-channel.md) and inspect the actual buying situation rather than counting attention alone. [Where customers and capability overlap](https://longform.asmartbear.com/first-marketing-channel/); [Interest without urgency](https://longform.asmartbear.com/purgatory/).

**Check whether value persists.** Read [product–market fit](../topics/product-market-fit.md) alongside [retention](../topics/retention.md). Acquisition, use, and continued value must tell a coherent story. [Sustained customer pull](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-market-fit/).

**Checkpoint:** Can the intended customer find, buy, adopt, and keep benefiting from the product? Diagnose the weakest step before scaling expenditure.
