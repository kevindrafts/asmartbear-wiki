# Build advantages that become harder to copy

A differentiating feature is not automatically a lasting defense. Cohen's moat discussion asks which accumulated assets or reinforcing activities protect the position: experience, ecosystem relationships, reputation, scale, and switching costs can work together. Naming the category is easier than explaining how the company will create it. [Durable advantage](https://longform.asmartbear.com/moats/); [Reinforcing capabilities](https://longform.asmartbear.com/leverage/).

Earned authority and industry knowledge can provide an early foundation, but they take time and do not ensure sales. A large audience helped Cohen recruit at WP Engine more than it generated launch purchases. The mechanism connecting an asset to a result needs evidence. [Hard-to-reproduce assets](https://longform.asmartbear.com/unfair-advantages/); [Reputation's actual contribution](https://longform.asmartbear.com/reputation/).

**Editorial application:** Specify what grows stronger with use, customers, or learning, and which investment builds it. Also inspect dependencies: a business whose access can be removed by a platform may be less defensible than it appears. Customer captivity can preserve revenue while increasing resentment and vulnerability. [Platform dependence](https://longform.asmartbear.com/marketing-platform-independence/); [Preference versus coercion](https://longform.asmartbear.com/willingness-to-pay/).
