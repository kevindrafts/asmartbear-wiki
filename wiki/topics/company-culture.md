# Culture appears in decisions that cost something

Values become useful when they guide a hard trade-off without requiring constant approval. Cohen distinguishes a company's enduring commitments from its changing products and operating methods. A statement that never affects behavior provides little guidance. [Values and organizational identity](https://longform.asmartbear.com/emptiness/); [Behavior as evidence](https://longform.asmartbear.com/show-dont-tell/).

Autonomy and coordination can conflict. Individual preferences deserve room, while shared obligations sometimes justify constraints. A good policy states the actual problem, limits the scope of standardization, and explains the benefits and burdens rather than maximizing administrative convenience by default. [Autonomy and administration](https://longform.asmartbear.com/tension-autonomy-admin/).

**Editorial application:** Review a recent difficult decision and ask which value changed the outcome. Invite strong disagreement about work while maintaining respect; productive tension differs from hostility. Growth itself does not demonstrate that the organization is treating people well, so define the outcomes success should support. [Constructive disagreement](https://longform.asmartbear.com/balance-vs-tension/); [Survival and values](https://longform.asmartbear.com/darwinian-company-growth/).

Keep the distinction between preserving principles and preserving every process as the company grows.

In [Nusii](../../processed/smart-bear-live-e13.md), Cohen describes a mission as something that would change what the company learns, builds, and communicates about customer outcomes. He allows that a company can discover such a commitment after founding, while insisting it must be sincerely held. **Editorial connection:** Ask which work the mission changes before judging the wording of its statement. The proposal-improvement examples are hypothetical product ideas, not established rules for winning deals. [E13 behavior implied by mission](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/13/transcript#t=58m27s); [A later mission](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/13/transcript#t=1h0m4s).
