# Validate the risky business assumption

Personal experience can reveal a real problem without proving a market. Cohen separates knowing how to build a solution from knowing whether others will choose it, pay enough, and remain customers. His startup risk model broadens the test to distribution, delivery, team capability, and endurance. [The founder's own problem](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scratched-my-own-itch/); [The chain of startup risks](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-drake-equation/).

Learning need not wait for a large product. A short project can test a clear pitch, reachable demand, or operating economics; later use supplies evidence interviews cannot. Conversely, a working application is weak validation if no relevant buyer sees it. [Commercial experiments](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-weekend/); [Different routes toward fit](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-market-fit-formula/).

**Editorial application:** Select the assumption whose failure would most change the venture, define what observation would challenge it, and build the least costly credible test. Preserve room to discover a better direction without assuming every poor initial idea will become a successful pivot. [Learning through an imperfect start](https://longform.asmartbear.com/your-idea-sucks-do-it-anyway/).
