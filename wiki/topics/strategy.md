# Strategy is a coherent set of costly choices

A strategy explains why this organization can win with these customers. Cohen distinguishes that explanation from an ambition, a roadmap, or a list of universally desirable qualities. A real choice has an attractive alternative and consequences the company agrees to accept. [What makes strategy useful](https://longform.asmartbear.com/great-strategy/); [Choices and consequences](https://longform.asmartbear.com/strategic-choices/).

Coherence emerges when choices reinforce each other. A small core and an extension ecosystem can support a small team, but also create compatibility and support obligations. Labeling the same fact a strength or weakness before choosing a direction hides this interaction. [Reinforcing choices](https://longform.asmartbear.com/strategic-choices/); [Why a SWOT list can mislead](https://longform.asmartbear.com/swot/).

**Editorial application:** Write the target customer, the reason to choose you, the capability making that promise credible, and the cost you accept. Test those decisions against actual product, sales, and staffing choices. Then translate the direction into a current plan with obstacles, actions, evidence, and deliberate exclusions. A clear plan executes strategy; it does not replace it. [From direction to work](https://longform.asmartbear.com/strategic-planning/).

In [Ymir](../../processed/smart-bear-live-e06.md), Cohen and Bryce Adams examine the tension between a critical infrastructure promise, a very low price, and reliance on one person. They propose combining software with paid implementation and training, so Alexander's expertise helps the customer's team operate independently. **Editorial connection:** Resolving a contradiction may require changing what is sold, not merely improving its description. This is a proposed business design, not evidence of a successful pivot. [E06 contradiction](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/6/transcript#t=24m58s); [Revised offer](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/6/transcript#t=40m10s).

[Mindstamp](../../processed/smart-bear-live-e10.md) adds a useful condition to the [Ymir](../../processed/smart-bear-live-e06.md) service idea: initial implementation work should enable continuing product use, rather than make every renewal depend on ongoing consulting. **Editorial comparison:** Evaluate what the service removes for the customer and what remains valuable afterward. Both conversations propose a direction rather than demonstrate its economics. [E10 services distinction](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/10/transcript#t=32m31s); [E06 training and implementation](https://smartbearlive.transistor.fm/6/transcript#t=40m10s).
