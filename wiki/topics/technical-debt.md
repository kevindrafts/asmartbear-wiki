# Engineering choices create future operating obligations

Fast early delivery can help a business learn while leaving quality and maintainability work for later. Cohen's mortgage analogy distinguishes recurring defect repair from changing the underlying causes. At scale, continuity and interacting components add costs that a simple duplication of resources will not solve. [Borrowing against future quality](https://longform.asmartbear.com/software-quality-mortgage/); [Robust systems cost more](https://longform.asmartbear.com/scale/).

Routine upkeep and large rework need different decision processes. Small fixes collectively improve quality, while a major investment should connect to incidents, delivery constraints, or an essential capability. Independent operational measurements help detect silent failures in the system used to judge that work. [Maintenance and strategic work](https://longform.asmartbear.com/rocks-pebbles-sand/); [Checking the monitoring system](https://longform.asmartbear.com/whos-lying/).

**Editorial application:** Explain the customer or delivery consequence of a technical liability, track the obligation, and choose a proportionate repair. Avoid blaming engineers for shortcuts the business previously demanded.

The corpus also includes a specialized logical-clock proposal, preserved in the catalog as technical work rather than forced into a startup slogan. Its ordering guarantees depend on stated assumptions and need the original paper and implementation for engineering use. [Distributed logical time](https://longform.asmartbear.com/distributed-logical-time/).
