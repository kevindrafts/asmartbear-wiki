# Connect customer needs, delivery, and business outcomes

Product work spans customer understanding, strategic choice, execution, and coordination. Cohen argues that expecting one person to excel at every part creates an impossible role; complementary capabilities across a team provide better coverage. [The impossible product manager](https://longform.asmartbear.com/great-product-manager/).

The central translation runs from a customer's situation to work that can be done, then to a credible contribution to business goals. That requires more than maintaining a backlog or accepting every feature request. Independent demand streams can preserve evidence from different functions while the team still makes one coherent execution plan. [The three languages](https://longform.asmartbear.com/three-languages/); [Organizing competing inputs](https://longform.asmartbear.com/jit-backlogs/).

**Editorial application:** Map responsibilities before hiring a generic PM. For each major initiative, record the customer outcome, strategic reason, constraints, owner, and evidence of progress. Match metrics to what the team can influence while keeping downstream results visible. A release is a delivery event, not proof that customer value or revenue followed. [Responsibility and measures](https://longform.asmartbear.com/product-metrics/).
