# Define success in the work as well as the outcome

Cohen describes fulfilling work as the overlap of enjoyment, competence, and organizational need. A title can outlive that overlap as a company grows; changing roles may preserve contribution better than insisting on the original job. [A role that fits](https://longform.asmartbear.com/fulfillment/).

Purpose also operates at different levels. The positive change a company seeks, its concrete future state, and the next milestone are related but distinct. A mission is useful when it influences decisions; the source explicitly does not establish that purpose alone improves profits. [Purpose, vision, and milestones](https://longform.asmartbear.com/mission-vision/).

**Editorial application:** Evaluate whether the daily work and the opportunities created for others remain worthwhile even if an anticipated exit does not occur. Give credit to the people who build and maintain the organization, rather than letting founder identity absorb their achievements. [The experience of building](https://longform.asmartbear.com/success-guaranteed/); [Shared contribution](https://longform.asmartbear.com/proud-of-founding/).

These are reflective criteria, not a guarantee of happiness or a substitute for economic viability. They help specify what a viable business is meant to support.
