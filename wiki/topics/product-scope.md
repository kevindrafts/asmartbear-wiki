# A small product that completes a useful job

Cohen's SLC proposal preserves fast learning while requiring the first release to deliver a narrow job end to end and offer a reason to enjoy using it. Completeness concerns the chosen promise, not every feature a competitor offers. [Simple, lovable, complete](https://longform.asmartbear.com/slc/).

The reason to buy may be unusually concentrated. A product can disappoint on secondary dimensions while solving one important need exceptionally well. But when trials do not convert, adding features without investigating abandoned attempts is an unsupported guess about the missing value. [The decisive benefit](https://longform.asmartbear.com/the-important-thing/); [Why non-buyers matter](https://longform.asmartbear.com/put-down-the-compiler/).

**Editorial application:** Specify the user, triggering situation, completed outcome, and minimum acceptable quality. Remove adjacent jobs before removing the steps needed to finish the core one. Decide which limitation customers knowingly accept and which would break the promise. Early shortcuts can create later maintenance obligations, so track what must be repaired if adoption grows. [The quality mortgage](https://longform.asmartbear.com/software-quality-mortgage/).
