# SaaS economics without false precision

Acquisition cost, account revenue, service margin, and retention describe different parts of a subscription business. Cohen objects to compressing unstable long-term assumptions into a confident lifetime-value number; inspecting the components and acquisition payback often makes action clearer. [The LTV critique](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ltv/).

Replacement acquisition is a real operating burden. The cost-of-cancellation model makes it visible, while steady-state earnings ask what remains if the company spends enough to maintain its revenue but stops buying additional growth. These are management models whose definitions and periods must stay consistent. [Replacement cost](https://longform.asmartbear.com/coc/); [Steady-state profitability](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ssebitda/).

**Editorial application:** Keep an explicit distinction among revenue, contribution after service costs, accounting earnings, and available cash. Include founder labor when judging an owner-operated business's sustainability. Neither a long-horizon positive return nor a positive adjusted metric guarantees the cash to reach it. [Founder compensation](https://longform.asmartbear.com/ramen-profitable/); [Collection timing](https://longform.asmartbear.com/annual-prepay/).

The practical question is which component can improve with evidence, not which combined score sounds most impressive. These historical essays provide reasoning tools, not current accounting or investment advice.
