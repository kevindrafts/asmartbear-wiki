# Choose customers by buying circumstances

A useful customer definition explains who values the product's trade-offs and why that person can buy now. Cohen's Carol is a decision aid, not an invented demographic biography. His later segmentation essay makes the same point through teams inside large companies: the buyer's workflow can matter more than the employer's size. [Ideal customer profile](https://longform.asmartbear.com/icp-ideal-customer-persona/); [Target market](https://longform.asmartbear.com/target-market/).

A segment also needs a reachable purchasing path. Enthusiasm without urgency leaves a product indefinitely postponed, while the broader market test asks whether the problem, budget, competition, and continued need fit together. Narrowing to a present buying trigger can be more productive than broadening the message. [Product purgatory](https://longform.asmartbear.com/purgatory/); [Problems and markets](https://longform.asmartbear.com/problem/).

**Editorial application:** Describe the buyer's situation, current alternative, trigger, constraints, and reason to prefer you. Test the description against wins, losses, and cancellations. Do not infer an enterprise strategy from a famous logo or treat the source's illustrative market multipliers as forecasts. Use the [official customer-profile workshop](https://skills.asmartbear.com/workshops/find-your-carol/) for Cohen's guided process.
