# What will additional capital actually change?

Capital can accelerate a known mechanism without supplying the missing insight behind it. Cohen asks founders to distinguish an identified team needing salaries from a team they have not found, and a working acquisition process from uncertainty about what customers buy. A credible case shows learning, not merely claims of adaptability. [What money can and cannot solve](https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-money/).

The amount required also depends on timing. Slower sales can leave costs uncovered, while faster growth can demand service and acquisition spending before collections arrive. A single expected cash trough ignores both risks. [Cash under different growth paths](https://longform.asmartbear.com/more-money/).

**Editorial application:** State the milestones funding would unlock, the evidence supporting each expense, and the assumptions still unresolved. Compare the day-to-day responsibilities and outcome expectations of funded and self-funded paths. If both are economically credible, personal preference can settle the choice; it cannot repair an unworkable model. [Choosing between viable paths](https://longform.asmartbear.com/business-tiebreaker/).

Fundraising success is not equivalent to lasting customer value. The corpus's historical critiques of unsustainable companies concern what gets built, across financing types. [Growth and durability](https://longform.asmartbear.com/unsustainable-companies/).
