# Let a recognizable voice follow real behavior

A human voice can distinguish a small company and help customers relate to it. Cohen connects forgiveness after a mistake with a history of care, competence, and repair—not simply the wording of an apology. [A relationship behind the message](https://longform.asmartbear.com/authentic/).

Calling a company authentic is weak evidence of authenticity. Concrete demonstrations and candid limits can be more persuasive than familiar adjectives. The social-media satire illustrates how mechanical attempts to appear genuine can become awkward or self-serving. [Claims need evidence](https://longform.asmartbear.com/authentic-is-dead/); [The satire of formulaic authenticity](https://longform.asmartbear.com/social-media-marketing/).

**Editorial application:** Choose a voice that fits the people and customers, then make policies and service consistent with it. A distinctive style may repel some buyers; decide whether they were your intended customers. Departing from convention can be viable, but brings its own costs and does not make conventional methods wrong. [An unconventional sales voice](https://longform.asmartbear.com/thats-the-way-its-done/).

Transparency is a choice with implications, not a universal duty to reveal every detail or a shortcut to trust.
