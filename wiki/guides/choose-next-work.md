# How do we choose what to build next?

**Use this when:** Every stakeholder has a credible priority and the team is busy without changing the business enough.

1. **State the current objective and obstacle.** Separate the longer-term strategy from what needs to change now. Investigate the problem before selecting a favored solution. [Connect the plan to the constraint](https://longform.asmartbear.com/strategic-planning/).
2. **Sort by decision type.** Distinguish urgent obligations, strategic commitments, tactical improvements, and routine quality work. Avoid forcing all of them through one ROI score. [Different rules for different work](https://longform.asmartbear.com/rocks-pebbles-sand/).
3. **Compare meaningful alternatives.** For large commitments, prioritize material impact and reject options with unacceptable weaknesses. For tactical work, use honest benefit and effort bands instead of false precision. [Choose consequential impact](https://longform.asmartbear.com/maximized-decision/); [Handle estimation error](https://longform.asmartbear.com/roi-rubric/).
4. **Schedule against reality.** Bring the strongest items from relevant input streams together with dependencies and actual capacity. A ranked wish list is not an executable plan. [Select just in time](https://longform.asmartbear.com/jit-backlogs/).
5. **Explain what will wait.** Name important exclusions and the cost accepted. Review evidence of outcomes after delivery, while retaining capacity for maintenance and follow-through. [Make deferral visible](https://longform.asmartbear.com/prioritization/).

**Leave with:** A small set of outcomes, owners, realistic capacity, observable progress, and explicit exclusions. If no major idea is sufficiently valuable, improve the options rather than spending months on the best of a weak set. Do not equate a high task-completion count with strategic progress.
