# How do I select an ICP?

**Use this when:** The product could serve several audiences, or customer requests are pulling it in incompatible directions. This is an editorial decision guide drawn from the sources below, not the author's full workshop.

1. **Describe the buying situation.** Name the job, current alternative, constraints, and desired result. Add company size or geography only where it changes those facts. A team at a large firm may buy like a small business. [Segment by circumstances](https://longform.asmartbear.com/target-market/).
2. **Look for a reason to act now.** Ask what project or event makes this problem important enough to displace another priority. Distinguish people who like the concept from people able and motivated to implement it. [Find present urgency](https://longform.asmartbear.com/purgatory/).
3. **Test the picture against people.** Compare strong customers, recent losses, and plausible prospects. Ask about behavior and purchasing rather than seeking approval for your proposed profile. [Revise the hypothesis](https://longform.asmartbear.com/customer-development/).
4. **Check economic and operational fit.** The segment needs reachable buyers, sufficient willingness and ability to pay, and requirements you can satisfy consistently. A famous logo or large theoretical market does not settle that. [Test the market conditions](https://longform.asmartbear.com/problem/).
5. **Choose the trade-offs.** Write the promise for one ideal buyer and identify requests you will decline. Watch whether adjacent buyers still recognize the benefit without diluting the message. [Use the profile to make decisions](https://longform.asmartbear.com/icp-ideal-customer-persona/).

**Leave with:** A short, evidence-backed buyer description, a list of unresolved assumptions, and a next test. Revisit it when losses among supposedly ideal customers reveal a mismatch. Do not treat the source's illustrative audience multipliers as a forecast.

For the author's guided process, use the [official customer-profile workshop](https://skills.asmartbear.com/workshops/find-your-carol/).
