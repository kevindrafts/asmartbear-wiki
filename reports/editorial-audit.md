# Editorial and citation audit

Snapshot: September 30, 2026.

- **Coverage:** 226 notes for 226 included articles; all source-body hashes match the complete bodies read. No missing notes, pending reviews, duplicated canonicals, or included extraction failures.
- **Citation structure:** Each note links directly to its canonical original. Every topic, guide, and reading path cites at least two distinct included primary sources. All local Markdown links and heading targets resolve; all staged pages are reachable from the homepage.
- **Substance:** Notes contain a concrete source argument, application, and boundary. The 226 notes range from 91–187 whitespace-separated words including links; 43 topics range from 182–242, ten guides from 271–319, and five reading paths from 140–169. These are descriptive counts, not quotas.
- **Quotes:** Original paraphrase is the default. Source article titles and occasional short attributed phrases remain third-party material. No extended block quotations or reproduced tables/diagrams are used. A normalized 30-word overlap scan against all source bodies reports zero findings across editorial Markdown, rendered HTML, and search entries. This automated guard does not itself establish originality or legal compliance; source-aware drafting and review supply the editorial check.
- **Models and numbers:** Notes retain assumptions rather than repeat dramatic headlines as universal promises. Particular checks cover the conditional revenue ceiling, churn and acquisition heuristics, annual-prepayment cash timing, forecast arithmetic, A/B testing assumptions, source chart/prose discrepancies, and the distinction between correlation and a demonstrated cause. Historical anecdotes are not presented as current benchmarks.
- **Tensions:** Synthesis preserves early one-priority advice versus the later product-metrics framework; acquisition versus retention as the binding growth constraint; founder autonomy versus organizational process; and persistence versus the cost of ignoring evidence. Satire, personal memoir, and technical material remain identified by context.
- **Dates:** Publication and modification metadata are preserved separately from sitemap dates. `growth-asymptote` and `swot` are publicly reachable but carry 2030 source dates; both notes and the coverage report explicitly flag this anomaly.
- **Diagrams and tables:** Complete extracted table cells, alt text, and captions were read along with article prose and footnotes. Notes preserve relevant conceptual meaning and qualifications in original words. No claim is made that every decorative image received pixel-level inspection; unresolved conflicts are qualified in notes rather than guessed.
- **Source links:** All 226 cited article destinations were successfully fetched under the crawl policy. Twenty legacy redirect chains were checked separately; one aliases an included article and 19 remain outside corpus scope. Excluded JSON and `/ref/` links are documented but deliberately not fetched. External third-party research linked by Cohen is not independently imported or validated as a second evidence corpus.
- **Rights:** Unofficial status is visible on the homepage and every footer. Original editorial licensing is separate from MIT code licensing and expressly excludes third-party articles, titles, quotations, images, and marks. Official workshops are linked only.

Machine results are retained in the repository as `reports/audit-results.json` and `reports/git-audit-results.json`. Repository review scans staged files and existing Git blobs for source passages, full-capture hashes, raw-capture paths, and recognizable credential patterns; it prints filenames only for findings.
